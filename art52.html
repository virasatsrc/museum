BRIGADIER MMS VASIR, AVSM (July 1984 - February 1986)

Brigadier Man Mohan Singh Vasir was commissioned into 5 SIKH on 12 June 1960. He was an Instructor at Indian Military Academy, Dehradun from December 1967 to January 1970. His other appointments include Brigade Major of a Brigade and GSO-2 in MT-2, AHQ. He raised 20 SIKH ('Towering Twenty') on 01 March 1976 and commanded the Battalion till July 1979. Later, he commanded two Infantry Brigades. His other appointments include DDG Financial Planning, AHQ, DDG Assam Rifles and ADG (B) NCC, before being promoted to the rank of Major General. He took over the Centre in one of the most trying circumstances in June 1984, and set about rehabilitating it and restoring its former image, to which his contribution was substantial.
