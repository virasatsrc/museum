BRIGADIER RAKESH SHARMA (September 2006 - March 2009)

Brigadier Rakesh Sharma was commissioned into 3 SIKH on 16 June 1974 and later commanded 10 SIKH at Mamun Cantonment. He held varied appointments including Brigade Major of a Brigade in Operation Pawan, GSO-2 in 10 Sector AR, GSO-2 in AHQ and Colonel General Staff in 18 RAPID. The officer also served in Assam Rifles as a Staff Officer and Additional Director. After commanding a Brigade in the deserts, he was the Commandant of Counter Insurgency & Jungle Warfare School, Wairangte, before coming to SIKH Regimental Centre as the Commandant. During his tenure at Centre, 'Ex-Maitree', the first Foreign Joint Training, was carried out with Thailand Army.
