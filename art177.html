Western Front

Title: Western Front
Category: Western Front
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
In the Chhamb sector, 5 Sikh fought an epic defensive battle at Mandiala South against Pakistan's armoured and infantry thrust. Lieutenant Colonel Prem Khanna and Major Jaivir Singh received the Maha Vir Chakra, while Lance Naik Richhpal Singh and Major D.S. Pannu received Vir Chakras, the latter posthumously. The battalion earned the Theatre Honour "Jammu and Kashmir 1971."

At Punch, 6 Sikh held Point 405 and the surrounding heights against repeated attacks by Pakistan's 12 Infantry Division. Its stand prevented the enemy from threatening the town. The battalion received the Battle Honour "Defence of Punch," the Theatre Honour "Jammu and Kashmir 1971," and eight gallantry awards, including the Maha Vir Chakra for Lieutenant Colonel K.L. Rattan and five Vir Chakras.

Under Lieutenant Colonel S. Chengapa, 9 Sikh advanced deep into the Lipa Valley, capturing Thandapani, Kaiyan Baihk, Mandakuli Heights, the Ved and Gyani complex and Three Pimples. Delta Company blocked the Mandal-Brithwari Gali road for seven days, and the battalion captured forty-six square kilometres of enemy territory.

In the Barmer sector, 10 Sikh under Lieutenant Colonel K.C. Kalley captured Ghazi Camp, Khokhropar, Apex Hill and Purbut Ali between 4 and 13 December. Purbut Ali brought the battalion six Vir Chakras, three Sena Medals, one Vishisht Seva Medal and six Mentions-in-Despatches. At Ajnala, 19 Sikh held the Darya Mansur and Budhai Chima posts, disrupted the enemy base and secured about 400 acres without losing territory. On 17-18 December, 2 Sikh recaptured Pul Kanjri. Lance Naik Shangara Singh silenced two machine guns despite fatal wounds and received the Maha Vir Chakra posthumously.
