LIEUTENANT GENERAL SS CHAHAL, PVSM, AVSM, VSM (August 2000 – June 2004)

An alumnus of 26th NDA course and 35th IMA Regular Course, Lieutenant General Sarvjit Singh Chahal, PVSM, AVSM, VSM was commissioned into 1 SIKH in June 1965. He saw action during the 1965 Indo-Pakistan War in the Tithwal-Uri sector with the Battalion.

His appointments include Second-in-Command of the raising team of 20 SIKH (March 1976), Commanding Officer 22 SIKH (November 1982 – July 1985) during the tumultuous period of 'Operation Blue Star', Commandant of the SIKH Regimental Centre (September 1990 – June 1991), and finally the Colonelcy of the Regiment from August 2000 to June 2004.

Lieutenant General SS Chahal is an alumnus of Defence Services Staff College, Wellington and Army War College (Higher Command). General Chahal commanded 11 Mountain Brigade (July 1991 – December 1993) and 25 Mountain Division (October 1996 – April 1998). His staff experience has been varied and long, from GSO-3 (Intelligence) at AHQ (1971-1974), Brigade Major 73 Mountain Brigade (January 1980 – March 1981), GSO-1 (Operations) 16 Infantry Division (July 1985 – June 1986), DDG Staff Duties AHQ (December 1993 – September 1996) and COS 4 Corps (May 1998 – November 2000). His instructional tenures include Instructor at Indian Military Academy Dehradun (July 1977 – December 1979), Instructor at Defence Services Staff College Wellington (July 1987 – June 1990) and Commandant of National Defence College, New Delhi (December 2002 – June 2004). A key highlight of his NDC command was the hosting of the 7th Meeting of the National Defence Universities and Colleges of the ASEAN Regional Forum.

He was awarded the Vishisht Seva Medal, Ati Vishisht Seva Medal and Param Vishisht Seva Medal in 1998, 2000 and 2003 respectively.
