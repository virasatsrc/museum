MAJOR GENERAL AJAY KUMAR SINGH (01 June 2024 – 31 December 2025)

Major General Ajay Kumar Singh, born on 06 Dec 1967 at Rishikesh, Uttarakhand, underwent his pre-commissioning training from the IMA, Dehradun, and was commissioned into 8 SIKH on 15 Dec 1990. The General Officer has excelled in all mandatory professional military courses, prominent among which are the YO Course, Cdo Course, Junior Command Course, Defence Services Staff College, Senior Command Course, Higher Command Course and National Defence College, achieving consistently high grading. He has also earned M.Phil Degrees in Def and Mgt Studies, in addition to an Executive MBA, further strengthening his strategic, operational and administrative expertise.

During his regimental service with 8 SIKH, the General Officer held multiple operational and administrative appointments in field, high-altitude and HAA/HAFA areas including service with the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon from Jun 2003 to Jan 2004. He commanded 14 SIKH from June 2007 to July 2009, leading the battalion in peace and operational roles with emphasis on combat readiness, training innovation and troop welfare.

He later commanded 70 Infantry Brigade at Leh from Jan 2017 to Nov 2018, leading a forward-deployed formation in a sensitive high-altitude and geopolitically significant sector. He subsequently served as General Officer Commanding, 5 Mountain Division at Rupa from May 2022 to Dec 2023. His staff experience includes important intelligence and operational appointments as General Staff Officer Grade 3 (Int) at HQ 181 Mountain Brigade from Apr 1999 to Apr 2000 and General Staff Officer Grade 1 (Ops) at Army HQ from Dec 2004 to Jun 2007.

The General Officer served as Senior Directing Staff (Army-1) at the National Defence College, New Delhi during which he assumed Colonelcy of The Sikh Regiment on 01 June 2024, a prestigious appointment reflecting his leadership, regimental ethos and organizational acumen, which he continues to hold till his superannuation on 31 December 2025. He is a recipient of the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal, Sena Medal, Chief of Army Staff Commendation Card, UN Medal and High-Altitude service awards.
