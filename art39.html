MAJOR GENERAL AJAY KUMAR SINGH, AVSM, SM (01 June 2024 – 31 December 2025)

Major General Ajay Kumar Singh, AVSM, SM was born on 06 December 1967 at Rishikesh, Uttarakhand. He was commissioned into 8 SIKH on 15 December 1990 after pre-commission training at the IMA, Dehradun. He served with the United Nations Interim Force in Lebanon (UNIFIL) and commanded 14 SIKH with emphasis on combat readiness and troop welfare.

He commanded 70 Infantry Brigade in the sensitive high-altitude sector of Leh, Ladakh and subsequently served as General Officer Commanding, 5 Mountain Division. He served as Senior Directing Staff (Army-1) at the National Defence College, New Delhi. On 01 June 2024, he assumed the prestigious appointment of Colonel of The SIKH Regiment, serving till 31 December 2025. He is a recipient of the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (AVSM), Sena Medal (SM), COAS Commendation Card, and UN Medal.
