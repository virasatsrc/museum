XLV Rattray's Sikhs

Title: XLV Rattray's Sikhs
Category: Regimental History
Regiment: Sikh Regiment

The XLV Rattray’s Sikhs, originally the Bengal Military Police Battalion, was raised on January 31, 1856, by Captain Thomas Rattray at Lahore. Initially comprising cavalry and ten infantry companies, the unit was composed of Sikhs, Punjabi Mussalmans, and Dogras. By 1862, the cavalry was detached, and non-Sikh elements were phased out by 1885, making the battalion a pure Sikh unit. The XLV served in the Santhal Parghanas in late 1856 during a time of widespread insubordination in the Bengal Native Infantry. Despite regional animosity following Punjab's annexation, the Sikh troops remained loyal and highly distinguished. The battalion was renamed the 3rd Battalion, 11th Sikh Regiment in 1922, and later redesignated the 3rd Battalion, The Sikh Regiment in 1945.
