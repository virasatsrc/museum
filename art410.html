Title: Battle of Barki (1965)
Category: Battle Gallery
Regiment: Sikh Regiment

On 07 September 1965, 4 SIKH under 7 Infantry Division was ordered to advance towards Lahore across the Ichhogil Canal in the Khemkaran Sector. Prior to this, on 06 September, it had to straighten the salient on the IB by capturing the Pakistani Border Outposts (BOPs) of Rakh Hardit Singh and Teja Singh Majra. Two companies simply moved across, surrounded and occupied the outposts and then handed them over to the Border Security Force (BSF). On 08 September, 4 SIKH was ordered to move North of the road Khalra-Barki-Lahore. The objective was to capture Barki in Phase 1 and in Phase 2, the Ichhogil Canal Bridge, which had fortified pill boxes with Heavy Machine Guns and mutually supporting Anti-Tank Guns. Barki, just 15 km short of Lahore, was to be captured by 4 SIKH with Heavy Tanks in assault role, after which 16 PUNJAB was to capture and demolish the bridge over Ichhogil Canal by midnight, and secure a Bridge-Head of 500 yards depth across the Western Bank.

Lieutenant Colonel Anant Singh, CO 4 SIKH, planned the capture of Barki in a single phase, with Alpha and Charlie Company groups. Their objectives were the Barki village and North of Barki, respectively. Bravo Company was to act as reserve, and Delta Company was grouped with a Squadron from the CENTRAL INDIA HORSE (CIH), for contingency tasks. On 10 September 1965, the assaulting companies moved to the Forming Up Place at 2000 hrs, but came under heavy enemy fire that opened up with mortars, artillery and automatics, from the village. Advance was difficult in the face of such concentrated fire, and the casualties were piling up. Undeterred, the assaulting companies moved towards their objectives without much difficulty, while Alpha Company, using fire and move tactics, fought through the concentrated enemy fire and reached West of Barki Village by 2100 hrs. Bravo Company then charged the enemy but came under heavy enemy artillery and Medium Machine Gun fire.

Major Shamsher Singh, the Company Commander, and Havildar Ajmer Singh, one of the Platoon Commanders, led the assault with great determination, and destroyed two pillboxes with Rocket Launchers, and eventually captured the village. The gallant actions of Subedar Ajit Singh were well recognized, and he was awarded the Maha Vir Chakra. Vir Chakras were awarded to Major Shamsher Singh, Havildar Ajmer Singh and Lance Naik Pritam Singh. At 2130 hrs, the enemy fired one of the heaviest artillery bombardments thus far witnessed in the war, some 5000 rounds in about half an hour, including those by Heavy and Super Heavy Guns. The following morning, Sabres of the Pakistan Air Force carried out extensive strafing around Barki, but casualties and damage to vehicles was minimal. 4 SIKH made heavy sacrifices during the Battle of Barki and was awarded the Battle Honour 'Barki', for its gallant actions.
