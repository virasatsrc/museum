Rise of the Sikh Kingdom

Title: Rise of the Sikh Kingdom
Category: Rise of the Sikh Kingdom
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
During the eighteenth century, the Sikh nation was divided into twelve Sardaris or misls, capable of fielding about 70,000 horsemen between the Indus and the Jamuna. Rivalry continued until the Shukarchakia misl gained ascendancy. In 1799, a century after the creation of the Khalsa, Maharaja Ranjit Singh established the first independent Sikh kingdom in Punjab. Acting in the name of the Gurus and the Khalsa, he united chiefs who resisted central authority. His government and army became known as the Sarkar Khalsaji.

Ranjit Singh expanded cautiously, securing each conquest before attempting another and avoiding premature conflict with the British. At his death in 1839, the kingdom stretched from the Sutlej to beyond Peshawar and the Khyber Pass, and from Sindh and Rajputana to Kashmir, Ladakh, Gilgit and Tibet, with Lahore as its capital.
