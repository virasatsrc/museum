From Korea to Gaza

Title: From Korea to Gaza
Category: From Korea to Gaza
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
India's commitment to United Nations peacekeeping began in 1953-54, when the Custodian Force (India) was sent to Korea to supervise the repatriation of prisoners of war. Sikh Regiment battalions later carried this tradition into some of the world's most sensitive regions. Following the Suez crisis of October 1956, the United Nations Emergency Force was established on the Egyptian side of the Gaza armistice line. Selected for this duty, 2 Sikh sailed from Bombay aboard S.S. Muzzafari on 27 October 1961 and deployed at Deir-el-Balh. Its task was to patrol and observe the Armistice Demarcation Line, prevent infiltration by either side and operate among mines, ravines, sand dunes and frequent sandstorms. No serious incident occurred in its area. On 16 April 1962, Lieutenant General Prem Singh Gyani presented UNEF Medals to the battalion and commended its initiative, judgement and vigilance.
