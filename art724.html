124 TA

Territorial Army (SIKH)

Unit Name: 124 TA
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: Territorial Army (SIKH)

Detailed Historical Record

"India Gate Terriers" was raised by Lt Col Bhagwant Singh Gill on 1 November 1956 with nucleus affiliated to The Rajputana Rifles. In May 1957, it was affiliated to The Sikh Regiment and reorganised as four urban and four provincial companies.

The battalion was embodied during the Indo-Pak Wars of 1965 and 1971, during which it provided protection to numerous vulnerable areas besides guarding prisoners-of-war camps.

During the Asian Games in 1982, the battalion played a significant role in organising the Games. The 1990s saw deployment of the battalion for the protection of sensitive Army and strategic installations. Its first major operational test came in January 1995, when the unit was embodied for Operation Rakshak in Baramulla, Jammu & Kashmir. On its own initiative, the battalion undertook 15 successful operations, resulting in a Letter of Appreciation from GOC 19 Infantry Division.

During Operation Vijay in 1999, the unit was tasked with protection of the IAF Radar Station at Anantnag, apart from protection of ammunition convoys and route-opening duties in the volatile areas of Anantnag District. The battalion foiled attempts by militants to disrupt convoy movement by recovering numerous IEDs and ensuring route safety. It also played an important role during Operation Parakram by providing protection to Headquarters Western Command and other sensitive tactical and strategic locations.

The battalion fielded a Republic Day contingent for the first time in 2006, and its immaculate performance received considerable appreciation.

On the occasion of the battalion's 55th Raising Day in November 2011, the Chief of Army Staff and Colonel of The Sikh Regiment conferred India's ace shooter Abhinav Bindra with the rank of Honorary Lieutenant Colonel, 124 (TA). Sachin Pilot, then a Minister in the Government of India, was commissioned as a Territorial Army officer in the unit on 6 September 2012 as a Lieutenant.

During the Territorial Army Day Parade in 2015, the battalion achieved a clean sweep by winning both the "Best Marching Contingent - 2015" and the "Best QG Trophy - 2015." It repeated its success by once again winning the Best QG Trophy in 2016.
