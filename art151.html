Operation VIJAY-Liberation of Goa, 1961

Title: Operation VIJAY-Liberation of Goa, 1961
Category: Operation VIJAY-Liberation of Goa, 1961
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
In December 1961, Portuguese forces attempted to delay the Indian advance into Goa by demolishing bridges and culverts, mining roads and creating obstacles in the hope that international intervention would secure a ceasefire. Their resistance collapsed within forty hours. On 18 December, 3 Sikh led the Eastern Thrust from Anmond after its patrols discovered heavy cratering and mining that prevented armour from advancing. Moving at 0400 hours, the battalion reached Mollem by 0730 hours and Dorbanda by evening, using civilian transport where available. On 19 December, its mounted column reached Margao through Sanvordem, while the marching column arrived at Dabolim airfield by 2100 hours. Although the main Portuguese force had surrendered, Captain R.S. Dahiya compelled nearly 400 soldiers to lay down their arms outside Margao on 20 December. Governor-General and Commander-in-Chief General Manuel Antorio Vassalo E. Silva surrendered unconditionally to Brigadier K.S. Dhillon. For its speed, discipline and conduct, 3 Sikh received the Theatre Honour "Goa."
