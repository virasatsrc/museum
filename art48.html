COLONEL RC PURI (July 1970 - October 1974)

Colonel Ramesh Chand Puri was commissioned on 12 September 1948 and commanded 7 SIKH from 1965 to 1968. He commanded the Sikh Regimental Centre from July 1970 to October 1974, steering the Centre with distinction through the historic 1971 Indo-Pak War. Under his stewardship, the Centre mobilized battalions, provided reinforcements, and organized hero welcomes for decorated war veterans.
