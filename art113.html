Gallipoli, 1915

Title: Gallipoli, 1915
Category: Gallipoli, 1915
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
XIV Sikhs, commanded by Colonel Philip Palin, moved from Egypt to Cape Helles on 30 April 1915. They fought in the Second and Third Battles of Krithia and later at Sari Bair. On 4 June, the battalion assaulted Turkish trenches astride Gully Ravine and penetrated to the third line, but suffered 371 officers and men killed or wounded-82 per cent of the assaulting strength. Havildar Maghar Singh and Uday Singh were among those distinguished. By December, XIV Sikhs had lost 27 British officers, 20 VCOs and about 1,000 men. Their gallantry brought thirty-five IDSMs in a single Gazette of India notification dated 28 July 1916, besides later awards of two DSOs, four Military Crosses, three IOMs and eight IDSMs.
