Title: Battle of Purbut Ali (1971)
Category: Battle Gallery
Regiment: Sikh Regiment

During 1971 Indo-Pakistan War, Purbut Ali was the last remaining enemy stronghold before the Indian Army could reach the green belt of Naya Chor in Pakistan. The feature was a bald hillock in a sandy wasteland 65 km inside enemy territory, held by two companies of 39 FRONTIER FORCE with one platoon of Mortars and four MMGs. 11 Infantry Division of Southern Command was concentrated in Barmer Sector of Rajasthan by October 1971 for operations towards Naya Chor. The division had two brigades and a T-55 tank squadron. The main attack was along axis Gadra Road-Munabao with subsidiary attacks on Khinsar and Chachro. 85 Infantry Brigade was tasked to undertake operations along the Munabao-Nayachor axis which was spearheaded by 10 SIKH, being commanded by Lieutenant Colonel KC Kalley.

Pakistan's principal defences in the Sector at Apex Hill and Purbut Ali were well dug-in and prepared. 10 SIKH was tasked to initially capture Ghazi Camp, a Border Out Post (BOP) and then the forward locality of Khokhropar. The Battalion was to then act as Advance Guard on axis Khokhropar-Naya Chor and establish a firm base for the brigade attack on Naya Chor. By first light of 04 December 1971, the Battalion was well dug-in at its Forward Assembly Area near Munabao. After heavy bombardment of Ghazi Camp and Khokhropar, 10 SIKH launched the attack on night 04/05 December, the enemy was taken by surprise and by 0600 hrs, Ghazi Camp was captured without much resistance. The advance was resumed without delay and by 0830 hrs, the township of Khokhropar was also cleared. By 11 December, Apex Hill was also captured - the next objective was the formidable feature of Purbut Ali, which was part of the main defences around Naya Chor. 10 SIKH launched the attack with three companies in two phases, with one company as Reserve. In Phase One, Delta and Bravo Companies were assigned the task of capturing Left Bump and Forward Spur by 0430 hrs, on 13 December 1971. In the Second Phase, Alpha Company was to pass through Bravo Company, which was held in reserve.

The fighting on the objective was heavy but by 0430 hrs, both the companies had captured their objectives. At 0515 hrs, Alpha Company passed through Bravo Company towards its objective, Depth Spur, where it literally collided with the incoming enemy counter-attack. With the enemy just 20 yards ahead, the men of the two forward platoons charged into the enemy with fixed bayonets. Surprised by the weight of the assault, the enemy wavered and fled. After this initial set back, the enemy returned, rather violently and launched repeated counter-attacks. However, Charlie Company under Major Amrik Singh Gill beat back enemy attempts to re-capture Purbut Ali.

Acts of valour were plenty - a wounded Major Amrik Singh Gill volunteered to move forward two of his platoons to beat back the counter attacks, Subedar Gurcharan Singh laid down his life while leading his platoon against a fierce enemy counter-attack and Naik Gurjant Singh made the supreme sacrifice in fierce hand-to-hand fighting. A young Sepoy, Mohan Singh bravely silenced an enemy Machine Gun which was threatening the flanks of the battalion attack. The complete operation lasted 14 days -10 SIKH was awarded the Battle Honour 'Purbut Ali' and it earned Six Vir Chakras, Three Sena Medals, One Vishist Seva Medal and Six Mention-in-Dispatches.
