BRIGADIER MML AHUJA, VSM (December 1986 - May 1988)

Brigadier Madan Mohan Lal Ahuja was commissioned into 5 SIKH on 07 June 1959. His various appointments include Staff Officer at HQ 1 Military Liaison Group, Nepal, Brigade Major of an Infantry Brigade, Instructor at College of Combat, GSO-1 and Colonel General Staff, Military Operations Directorate, AHQ and DDG Military Intelligence. He commanded 5 SIKH from February 1975 to December 1977 and D&O Sub Area from May 1990 to December 1992. During his tenure as Centre Commandant, the 7th Post War Reunion was held at Ramgarh from 25 February 1988 to 27 February 1988.
