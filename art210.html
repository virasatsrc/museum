Jhelum and Nowshera

Title: Jhelum and Nowshera
Category: Jhelum and Nowshera
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
The Sikh Regimental Centre began at Jhelum in undivided Punjab, where XXXV Sikhs was converted into the Training Battalion of the new Sikh Regiment. Depot companies of the active battalions assembled there, and the new organisation assumed responsibility for recruiting, training, attesting and providing reinforcements to the Regiment. In March 1922, HRH the Prince of Wales presented new Colours to the Training Battalion. Jhelum, however, lacked adequate training and administrative facilities, and the Centre moved to Nowshera in December 1922 after a stay of only sixteen months.

Nowshera, associated with the Khalsa victory over the Afghans in 1823 and Hari Singh Nalwa's frontier command, provided the required infrastructure. The Training Battalion consolidated there under Colonel J.C. Freeland, its first Commandant in 1922-23, and developed barracks, institutions and traditions that served the Regiment until 1947. By the outbreak of the Second World War, the 10th Battalion had evolved into a firmly established Sikh Regimental Centre.
