COLONEL SS CHAHAL (September 1990 - June 1991)

Colonel Sarvjit Singh Chahal was commissioned into 1 SIKH on 27 June 1965, just before Pakistan's Operation Gibraltar. He saw action within months of his joining the Unit, and was subsequently involved in the Unit's Mechanization. By March 1976, when 20 SIKH was ordered to be raised, he was among its pioneers. Among the various staff appointments he held were GSO-3 (Intelligence), AHQ, Brigade Major, 73 Mountain Brigade, GSO-1 (Operations) 16 Infantry Division, DDG Staff Duties, AHQ, COS, 4 Corps, ADGMO and DGMO. He was an Instructor at Indian Military Academy and Defence Services Staff College, Wellington. He commanded 22 SIKH, 11 Mountain Brigade and 25 Infantry Division. He was the Commandant of the prestigious National Defence College at New Delhi.
