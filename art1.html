GURU NANAK DEV JI

The founder of Sikh faith was born on 15 April 1469 at Talwandi village near Lahore (now in Pakistan) to Kalyan Chand and Mata Tripta. Right from his birth, Guru Nanak had a religious bent of mind - his later travels to major religious centers worldwide helped crystallise his own ideas and give sharper definition to the contours of a new religion - Sikhism. Impressed by the basic compassion of Hinduism and the essential brotherhood of Islam, Guru Nanak professed equality of all humans in the sight of God. He instituted the Gurmukhi Script ('from the Guru's Mouth') to communicate with his followers. Guru Nanak's 974 hymns form the significant part of Guru Granth Sahib, which was compiled 65 years after his death. He did not appoint a successor from his two sons but chose Bhai Lehna (later, Guru Angad Dev Ji), who was his devout follower, to take his teachings forward. Guru Nanak died at Kartarpur on 07 September 1539.
