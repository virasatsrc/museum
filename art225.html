Boxing, Wrestling and Wider Achievement

Title: Boxing, Wrestling and Wider Achievement
Category: Boxing, Wrestling and Wider Achievement
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
Boxing rose from Havildar Joginder Singh's national title in 1960 to international distinction. Naik Bakshish Singh and Havildar Major Singh succeeded at the 1974 Asian Games. Subedar Kaur Singh became a four-time national champion, won Asian Games gold in 1982, received the Padma Shri and Arjuna Award and represented India at the 1984 Los Angeles Olympics. Naib Subedar Gurcharan Singh competed at the 1996 Atlanta and 2000 Sydney Olympics, while Manpreet Singh won Asian Games silver in 2010. In wrestling, Subedar Madho Singh placed fourth at the 1960 Rome Olympics after winning two Asian Games gold medals, while Subedar Jit Singh, Hind Kesri, competed at Tokyo in 1964 and won Commonwealth and Asian medals.

The Regiment also produced notable swimmers, mountaineers, rowers, shooters, basketball and football players. Lieutenant Colonel J.C. Joshi joined the successful 1965 Everest expedition; Colonel D.N. Tankha led the 1971 Nun Kun expedition; and Ranjit Singh won two Asian rowing silvers in 2010. The Regimental Centre shooting team won the ARA Tournament and President's Banner in 1967.

The chapter's spirit is best represented by Sepoy Darbara Singh of 9 Sikh, who fractured his knee during the 1966 General Choudhary Trophy at Mhow but completed the course, carried by his comrades over every obstacle. The team finished eight minutes ahead, earning the Chief of Army Staff's praise for its exceptional teamwork and comradeship.
