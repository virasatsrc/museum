BRIGADIER APS PAKIANATHAN, VSM** (December 2001 - October 2002)

Brigadier Albert Philip Samuel Pakianathan was commissioned into 17 SIKH (TIGERS) in August 1976. During his distinguished career, he held various appointments to include Brigade Major of an Infantry Brigade, GSO-1 (Training) at Indian Military Academy, Chief Instructor at Special Frontier Force Academy, Chakrata and Instructor at Air War College, Secunderabad. He commanded 18 SIKH from 1997 to 1999, and then again from 2002 to 2003. The Unit celebrated its Silver Jubilee during his command, and was also adjudged the 'Best Battalion' of the Division. As Commandant SRC, he judiciously oversaw the implementation of Phase II of KLP. He was instrumental in enhancing the security of the area by creating a boundary wall, and thus reducing the cattle menace to a great extent. A recipient of the Vishisht Seva Medal twice, he also contributed significantly to enhance the natural beauty and ambience of the Centre.
