Early Distinction and Overseas Performances

Title: Early Distinction and Overseas Performances
Category: Early Distinction and Overseas Performances
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
The bands soon gained reputations beyond their battalions. XXXVI Sikhs participated in the Prince of Wales's Rawalpindi review on 21 November 1905 and later in the Delhi Coronation ceremonies. XLV Rattray's Band appeared at the Edinburgh Military Tattoo in 1905, was judged the Indian Army's best band in 1910 and performed at the Coronation Durbar of King George V in 1911. XIV Sikhs won first prize against twenty-five other bands in the Bengal Native Army Band Competition in 1910 and later toured Singapore, Rangoon and the Philippines.

Before the Second World War, the 4/11 Sikh Band served at major state ceremonies in Delhi under Subedar Gulab Singh. In 1946, the Sikh Regiment Military Band toured Burma and Malaya. After Independence, 4 Sikh and its band were judged best at the Prime Minister's Parade at Jalandhar on 20 February 1948.
