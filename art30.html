LIEUTENANT GENERAL NPS BAL, AVSM (May 1990 – June 1993)

Lieutenant General Naresh Pal Singh Bal was commissioned in 16 CAVALRY in December 1955, and saw action during the Indo-Pakistan War of 1965 in the Ferozepur Sector. A graduate from Defence Services Staff College, Wellington, he was Brigade Major of 93 Infantry Brigade, after which in 1970, he volunteered for the SIKH Regiment, and served as Second-in-Command, 4 SIKH. He saw action with 4 SIKH during the Indo-Pakistan War of 1971. He commanded 4 SIKH from August 1972 to March 1975.

He served as Deputy GOC of an Infantry Division in the Northern Sector, from September 1981 to February 1984. He was Commanding MP Sub Area during Operation Blue Star. He commanded 3 Infantry Division, and on elevation to the rank of Lieutenant General, he took over the command of Indian Military Academy, Dehradun on 22 June 1989, where he remained till 19 May 1990. He also commanded 33 Corps for a short period and his last assignment before retirement was Director General, Rashtriya Rifles.

He assumed the appointment of Colonel of The SIKH Regiment in May 1990. He was awarded the Param Vishisht Seva Medal in 1990, for his meritorious and distinguished service in the Indian Army.
