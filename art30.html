LIEUTENANT GENERAL NPS BAL, AVSM (May 1990 – June 1993)

Lieutenant General Naresh Pal Singh Bal, AVSM was commissioned into 16 CAVALRY in December 1955 and soon transferred to 1 SIKH. He commanded 1 SIKH from 1974 to 1976. He subsequently commanded 59 Mountain Brigade and 28 Infantry Division in Counter-Insurgency operations in the North-East.

As Lieutenant General, he served as Chief of Staff, Eastern Command and Director General of Military Intelligence (DGMI) at Army Headquarters. He served as Colonel of the SIKH Regiment from May 1990 to June 1993. He was decorated with the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (AVSM) for his distinguished leadership.
