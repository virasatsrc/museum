6 RR

Rashtriya Rifles (SIKH)

Unit Name: 6 RR
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: Rashtriya Rifles (SIKH)

Detailed Historical Record

Raising

6th Battalion, The Rashtriya Rifles was raised on 15 October 1990 at Saugor by Lt Col CPS Mahlawat of the Mahar Regiment. The battalion was raised as part of HQ 2 Sector RR and underwent its initial six months of training from 15 October 1990 to 11 April 1991 at the Mahar Regimental Centre.

Operation Rakshak - Punjab

On completion of its raising, the battalion moved to Khasa, Amritsar, on 12 April 1991 and was deployed in Punjab for border sealing duties and counter-insurgency/counter-terrorism operations during the Punjab militancy.

During this tenure, the battalion eliminated 54 terrorists and recovered 67 weapons. Its professionalism was recognised by the Chief of Army Staff Unit Citation on 15 January 1994.

Operation Rakshak - Jammu & Kashmir

The battalion was inducted into the Kashmir Valley on 17 April 1994 under Col SS Uppal. It was initially deployed at Tral, Pulwama, and subsequently moved to Pahalgam, Anantnag, on 14 July 1994 under HQ 2 Sector RR, with Battalion Headquarters located at Bijbehara/Deosar.

The battalion continued its successful operations in Anantnag District till 22 May 1999. During this period, the affiliation of the battalion was permanently fixed with The Sikh Regiment, bringing a positive change in the character and ethos of the battalion.

The unit earned the appreciation of the GOC-in-C, Northern Command for its outstanding performance. On 22 May 1999, the battalion moved to North Kashmir, leaving behind a record of 50 terrorist kills and 83 apprehensions in the area.

After transiting through Gulmarg and Sopore, where it served for approximately four months, the battalion moved to Vilgam, Kupwara District, on 25 August 1999.

Immediately on arrival, the battalion set out to clear heavily terrorist-infested areas with determination and vigour. Effective domination over terrorist activities helped create a sufficiently peaceful environment for the incident-free conduct of the State Assembly Elections in September 2002, during which a 60 percent voter turnout was recorded.

The battalion continued to perform beyond expectations, achieving success after success. In recognition of its superlative performance, it was awarded the Northern Command GOC-in-C Appreciation on 15 January 2013.

The battalion maintained its dominance over the Rawar and Ramhal blocks, accounting for 251 terrorist kills, 170 apprehensions and 14 surrenders, besides recovering approximately 300 weapons and large quantities of explosives, weapons and detonators.

Apart from counter-insurgency and counter-terrorism operations, the battalion also carved a niche for itself in Sadbhavana, WHAM and PM activities.

After an unprecedented tenure under 7 Sector RR, the unit was relocated to the Kachhal Sub-Sector in High Altitude Area along the Line of Control, under the aegis of HQ 268 Infantry Brigade, from 31 December 2011.

The battalion was inducted during the winter season into snow-clad terrain, but the severe weather and difficult terrain did not affect the courage and determination of the "Soaring Six."

During a period of approximately two and a half years in the Kachhal Sub-Sector, the battalion participated actively in numerous operations and executed various Sadbhavana and PM activities. During this tenure, the unit made recoveries including approximately 30 weapons, 1,621 rounds of ammunition, 164 grenades and Rs 8,37,500, besides effecting apprehensions.

The unit also ensured zero infiltration in its entire Area of Responsibility and successfully foiled two BAT attempts as well as infiltration attempts.

In the highest traditions of the Army, the battalion has stood as a beacon of professionalism and courage, upholding the high standards of The Sikh Regiment, and continues to do so.
