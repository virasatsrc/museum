16 RR

Rashtriya Rifles (SIKH)

Unit Name: 16 RR
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: Rashtriya Rifles (SIKH)

Detailed Historical Record

16 Rashtriya Rifles Battalion (Sikh) was raised in May 1994 at MEG Centre, Bangalore, under HQ 6 Sector Rashtriya Rifles. The battalion was initially affiliated with the Punjab Regiment, but this affiliation was changed to The Sikh Regiment on 16 September 1994, a date which the battalion now celebrates as its Raising Day.

After successful training and validation at the Counter Insurgency and Jungle Warfare School, Vairengte, the battalion was deployed for election duties in Manipur in 1995 and subsequently moved to Nagaland for Operation Orchid.

The battalion was inducted into Operation Rakshak in Jammu & Kashmir in June 1999. At a time when insurgency was at its peak, it moved into the turbulent areas of Surankote, J&K. The battalion not only effectively controlled insurgency and militancy in the region, but was also instrumental in bringing the situation down to its lowest level during the period from July 1999 to July 2003. Over its rich and glorious innings of 25 years, the battalion has remained at the forefront of counter-insurgency and counter-terrorism operations in the Jammu Region.

In keeping with the Sikh Regiment motto "Nische Kar Apni Jeet Karon" and the Rashtriya Rifles motto "Dridhta and Veerta", the personnel of the battalion have written tales of glory through courage, sacrifice and professional excellence.

The battalion has also remained at the forefront of humanitarian assistance and aid to civil authority. In 2016, when Jammu & Kashmir was affected by floods, the battalion carried out extensive rescue and relief operations for the stranded local population.

For its sterling contribution in counter-terrorist operations, the battalion was awarded the COAS Unit Citation in 2003 and the GOC-in-C Unit Appreciation in May 2005.

A First Day Cover was released on the occasion of the battalion's Silver Jubilee on 16 September 2019.
