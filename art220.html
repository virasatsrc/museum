Colours, Veterans and the Move to Ramgarh

Title: Colours, Veterans and the Move to Ramgarh
Category: Colours, Veterans and the Move to Ramgarh
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
The fourth Reunion, from 2 to 4 March 1968, became a milestone when President Dr Zakir Hussain presented new Colours to the Regiment at Meerut. The fifth, held on 7-8 March 1975, was the last at Meerut. Major General Satinder Singh was Colonel of the Regiment, and about 500 pensioners attended, including Subedar Major and Honorary Captains Gian Singh, Victoria Cross, and Karam Singh, Param Vir Chakra. Lieutenant General R.A. Savory, Major R. Henderson, Captain Sheriff Frank Middleton and Captain A.C.B. Wimbush travelled from Britain, continuing the old Indo-British regimental association.

The sixth Reunion, from 26 February to 1 March 1979, was the first at Ramgarh Cantonment. It brought together veterans including Lieutenant General W.L. Farrow, Major J.M.S. Smith and Captain Wimbush. The seventh was delayed for nine years by Operation BLUE STAR and its aftermath and was finally held from 25 to 27 February 1988. Lieutenant General P.S. Vadehra inaugurated Bal Bhavan, reaffirming the Regiment's responsibility towards its children.
