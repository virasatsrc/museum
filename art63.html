BRIGADIER RK GUPTA, VSM (October 2002 - June 2004)

Brigadier Ravi Kant Gupta was commissioned into 10 SIKH in November 1971 and served in the 1971 War in Punjab. He commanded 10 SIKH in intense Counter Insurgency Operations in Jammu and Kashmir. He commanded the Sikh Regimental Centre from October 2002 to June 2004, receiving the Vishisht Seva Medal. He modernized firing ranges, introduced IT in administration, and constructed the iconic Khalsa Dwar.
