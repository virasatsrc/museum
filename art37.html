LIEUTENANT GENERAL SK JHA, PVSM, AVSM, YSM, SM (November 2015 – January 2020)

Lieutenant General Sanjay Kumar Jha, PVSM, AVSM, YSM, SM was born on 14 January 1960 at Madhubani, Bihar. An alumnus of Sainik School Tilaiya, he joined NDA in January 1977 in Hunter Squadron. He excelled in both academics and sports at school, NDA and IMA. He was commissioned into 17th Battalion The Sikh Regiment on 13 December 1980.

In Young Officers' course, he was 'Overall Best Student', later also earning the coveted 'Dagger' in Commando Course. He has been 'Best Student' in BSW, CIJW and Junior Command Courses. He is a graduate of Defence Services Staff College Wellington, Higher Command Course and the prestigious National Defence College, New Delhi.

He commanded 16 RR Battalion (SIKH) at Surankote (Poonch) in intense Counter-Terrorist Operations, where the unit was awarded the COAS Unit Citation and he was awarded the Sena Medal. Thereafter, he commanded 9 Sector AR, 21 Mountain Division and 33 Corps. His illustrious staff appointments include Brigade Major of an Infantry Brigade, GSO-1 (Operations) in a Mountain Division, Director Military Operations at AHQ, Brigadier General Staff (Operations) in a Command Headquarters and Director General Perspective Planning in AHQ. He has also been a Deputy Commander in the UN Mission at Congo.

He has been Instructor at Indian Military Academy Dehradun, HQ IMTRAT Bhutan and Senior Command Wing, Army War College Mhow. He took over as the 48th Commandant of Indian Military Academy, Dehradun on 03 September 2017.

The General Officer assumed Colonelcy of the SIKH Regiment on 01 November 2015 and remained at the helm till his superannuation in January 2020.
