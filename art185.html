Operation PAWAN-Sri Lanka

Title: Operation PAWAN-Sri Lanka
Category: Operation PAWAN-Sri Lanka
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
Following the Indo-Sri Lankan Accord of July 1987, the Indian Peace Keeping Force entered Sri Lanka to disarm the LTTE. When fighting began, 16 Sikh under Colonel A.K. Chatterjee was airlifted to Palaly on 15 October and entered battle the next day. It linked with the Jaffna Fort garrison on 20 October, captured Jaffna town and later operated in Trincomalee, Vavuniya and Mullaitivu, where it captured a fortified LTTE camp. It returned in March 1989 with two Vir Chakras and two Sena Medals.

Twenty-two Sikh arrived in October 1987 and fought at Vavuniya, Pulmoddai, Oddusudan and Nedunkerni. Sepoy Gurdip Singh received the Vir Chakra posthumously during election-security operations. Seventeen Sikh entered Velvettithurai in October 1988, while 7 Sikh fought around Nedunkeni with artillery and Mi-25 helicopter support. Operations were suspended on 20 September 1989.
