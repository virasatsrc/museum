Raising of New Battalions

Title: Raising of New Battalions
Category: Raising of New Battalions
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
6 Sikh, known as "Chhe," was raised at Meerut on 9 February 1962 by Lieutenant Colonel Gurbakhsh Singh, drawing officers, JCOs and men from across the Regiment. Major General B.W. Key, CB, DSO, MC, a former Colonel of the Regiment, welcomed the expansion and expressed the hope that the new battalion would become the finest in the world.

The earlier 7 Sikh having been redesignated 5 Sikh, a new 7 Sikh was raised on 1 January 1963. Lieutenant Colonel Bhagat Singh became its first commanding officer in April and gave the battalion its enduring ethos, "To the Top the Hard Way." On the same date, 8 Sikh was raised from a small nucleus assembled by Major Gulcharan Singh. Lieutenant Colonel J.S. Sandhu assumed command on 21 March 1963, and the battalion moved to Palampur in July for intensive mountain-warfare training. It adopted the title "Charhdi Kala," symbolising high spirits.

The raising of 9 Sikh began at Meerut on 1 April 1964 under Major R.T. Morlin. Lieutenant Colonel Harbans Singh took command on 17 September 1964, after which the battalion moved to Rangiya for jungle-warfare training. Together, these new units significantly enlarged the Regiment and prepared it for the changing operational environment.
