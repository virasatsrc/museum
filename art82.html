Creation of the Order of the Khalsa

Title: Creation of the Order of the Khalsa
Category: Creation of the Order of the Khalsa
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
The murder of Guru Arjun Dev in 1606 by the Mughals marked a decisive change. Guru Har Gobind prepared the Sikhs to resist persecution and established the principle of miri-piri, combining temporal and spiritual authority. Guru Gobind Singh completed this transformation by uniting faith, moral discipline, sacrifice and military duty. He taught his followers to practise arms, never turn their backs in battle and surrender life rather than faith.

On 30 March 1699, during Baisakhi at Anandpur Sahib, Guru Gobind Singh called for volunteers willing to sacrifice themselves for Dharma. Five men from different castes and regions came forward. Through Pahul, the Initiation of the Double-edged Sword, they became the Panj Piare, the first members of the Khalsa, and received the common title "Singh." The Guru then asked them to initiate him, becoming the sixth Khalsa. The new fraternity was distinguished by Kesh, Kangha, Kacch, Kara and Kirpan, symbols of discipline, identity and readiness to defend righteousness against oppression.
