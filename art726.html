157 TA

Territorial Army (SIKH)

Unit Name: 157 TA
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: Territorial Army (SIKH)

Detailed Historical Record

157 Infantry Battalion (TA) (H&H) Sikh, also known as the "Jammu Terriers," is the second youngest battalion of The Sikh Regiment. It was raised at Ranchi on 14 January 2004 by Col N. C. Bhatt. Personnel from the districts of Jammu, Rajouri, Udhampur, Reasi and Akhnoor form part of the battalion.

In the war against terrorism, the unit continues to operate in its own home and hearth environment, providing a secure living environment to the local population. The battalion carries out extensive intelligence and surveillance activities in its Area of Responsibility and provides continuous valuable intelligence regarding over-ground workers, sleeper cells and anti-national elements. In the event of war, the unit is also tasked for employment on rear-area duties.

On 14 February 2013, acting as guides and trackers, the battalion participated in a joint operation launched in the forests of Sarsoti, Jammu & Kashmir. After a prolonged search, a large cache of arms and ammunition was recovered.

Later, in November 2014, the unit achieved success during Operation Kathar, neutralising terrorists and recovering a substantial quantity of arms and ammunition.

The battalion has also produced outstanding sportsmen who have brought laurels to both the nation and the unit. Sep Nittin Kumar Sharma, a handball goalkeeper, won a gold medal at the 17th Asian Games in 2014 in South Korea and also participated in the 3rd Asian Men's Handball Championship in 2014. Sep Parveen Singh won a gold medal in the 23rd WFSKU Open International Karate Championship in 2018.

In 2015, the battalion was adjudged the Best Territorial Army Battalion and was awarded the coveted COAS Silver Trophy.

For its exemplary performance, the unit also received the GOC-in-C Western Command Unit Appreciation in 2015 and has been awarded the J&K Governor's Appreciation twice.
