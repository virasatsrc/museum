LIEUTENANT GENERAL MS BHULLAR, PVSM, VSM (June 1993 – July 2000)

Lieutenant General Manjit Singh Bhullar, PVSM, VSM was born at Sudhar in Ludhiana, Punjab. Commissioned into 7 SIKH in August 1961, he saw combat in the 1962 conflict and the 1965 Indo-Pak War. He commanded 7 SIKH in high altitude and served as Brigade Major during the 1971 war.

He commanded 68 Mountain Brigade in Nagaland, 23 Infantry Division, and 11 Corps. During Operation Vijay (Kargil War 1999), he served as Chief of Staff, Western Command, ensuring flawless operational logistics and readiness. He was Colonel of the SIKH Regiment from June 1993 to July 2000, guiding the regiment through the Saragarhi Centenary (1997) and Kargil victory. For supreme devotion to duty, he was awarded the PVSM and VSM.
