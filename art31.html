LIEUTENANT GENERAL MS BHULLAR, PVSM, VSM (June 1993 – July 2000)

Lieutenant General Manjit Singh Bhullar was born at Sudhar in Ludhiana, Punjab. He joined Alpha Squadron, 18th NDA course in July 1957 where he excelled in both academics and sports. After passing out of Indian Military Academy, Dehradun, he joined 2 SIKH on 11 June 1961 and served in the United Nations Force in Gaza, which was then part of United Arab Republic. As a young officer, he captained the Sikh Regimental Hockey Team and won the Obaidullah Khan Gold Cup in 1964 at Bhopal. A member of the pioneering team that raised 9 SIKH in May 1964, he saw action during the Indo-Pakistan Wars of 1965 and 1971 with the Battalion. A graduate of Defence Services Staff College, he commanded 9 SIKH during Anti-Insurgency Operations in Nagaland where he was awarded the Vishisht Seva Medal for distinguished services of exceptional order. After command, he attended the Higher Command Course in 1981-82.

He has been Instructor at College of Combat, Commander 116 Infantry Brigade, Deputy GOC 2 Mountain Division, GOC 14 Infantry Division, and GOC 16 Corps. His notable staff assignments were Brigade Major 86 Infantry Brigade, Colonel General Staff 23 Infantry Division, Brigadier Administration HQ 10 Corps, Major General General Staff HQ Central Command, and Quarter Master General of the Indian Army.

His notable contributions as Colonel of The SIKH Regiment include approval of the present KLP of SRC Ramgarh, raising the Regimental Hockey Team (which won many national tournaments subsequently), publication of the Address Book, Valour Book, Skippers' and Sportsmen Book and grand conduct of Sesquicentennial Celebrations, Battle of Saragarhi Centenary and 10th Post War Reunion. At national level, he was the President of Equestrian Federation of India and Vice President of Indian Hockey Federation.
