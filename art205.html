LANCE NAIK KARAM SINGH, PVC

Title: Lance Naik Karam Singh, PVC
Category: Hall of Fame
Award: Param Vir Chakra
Era / Action: Indo-Pakistan War, 1947–48
Citation (Written):

(30k,' Lance Naik Karam Singh, PVC (Indo-Pakistan War, 1947-48) Tithwal in Jammu and Kashmir was captured on 23 May 1948. After that, the enemy made numerous attempts to re-capture Richhmar Cali and Tithwal. On 13 October 1948, coinciding with Eid, the enemy decided to launch a brigade attack to retake Richhmar Gali, bypassing Tithwal and advance into the Srinagar Valley. Lance Naik Karam Singh was commanding a section in this area. The enemy commenced the attack with heavy shelling of Guns and Mortars. The fire was so accurate that not a single bunker in the platoon locality was left unscathed. Communication trenches caved in. Bravely, Lance Naik Karam Singh went from bunker to bunker, giving SUCCOUr to the wounded and urging the men to fight. The enemy launched eight separate attacks that day. In one such attack, the enemy managed to obtain a foothold in the platoon locality. Immediately Lance Naik Karam Singh, who was severely wounded by then, with a few men, hurled himself in a counter-attack and evicted the enemy after a close quarter encounter which accounted for many enemy dead, having been bayoneted by the brave Khalsas. Lance Naik Karam Singh proved himself to be a dauntless and born leader of men in crisis. Nothing could subdue him and no amount of fire or hardship could break his spirit. His gallant actions on that day inspired his comrades to face the massive onslaught unflinchingly. It was his fiercely proud fighting spirit which was largely responsible for the gallant stand at Tithwal that day.
