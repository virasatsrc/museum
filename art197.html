SEPOY ISHAR SINGH, VC

Title: Sepoy Ishar Singh, VC
Category: Hall of Fame
Award: Victoria Cross
Era / Action: Waziristan Campaign, 1921
Citation (Written):

Sepoy IShar Singh, VC (North-West Frontier, 1921) On 10 April 1921 at Haidary Kach (Waziristan) when the convoy protection troops were attacked, Sepoy IShar Singh was No I of a Lewis Gun Section. Early in the action, he had received a severe gun shot in the chest and fell beside his Lewis Gun. Several of his comrades were killed or wounded in hand-to-hand fighting, and his gun was seized by the enemy. Calling up to other men, he got up, charged on the enemy, recovered his gun and though bleeding profusely, again got the gun into action. He was ordered to go back and have his wounds dressed. Sepoy IShar Singh went to the Medical Officer, and despite his wounds, was of great assistance to him. He made innumerable journeys to the river and back for carrying water to the wounded. On one occasion, when enemy fire was very heavy, he took the rifle from a wounded man and helped to keep the fire down. He also shielded the Medical Officer who was dressing the wounded. His gallantry and devotion to duty were beyond praise.
