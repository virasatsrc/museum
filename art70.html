BRIGADIER M SRI KUMAR, SC (February 2020 - April 2022)

Brigadier M Sri Kumar was commissioned into 7 SIKH on 16 December 1989. The officer has held various appointments in 7 SIKH, including Second-in-Command and subsequently commanded 7 SIKH. He also held an instructional appointment at National Defence Academy, Khadakwasla as a Major. Later, he commanded 32 Infantry Brigade. During his illustrious service, the officer has served as Brigade Major in HQ 100 Mountain Brigade, Staff Officer in COR The SIKH Regiment Secretariat, Defence Attaché and Brigadier Administration, NDA, Khadakwasla.

The officer has been awarded Shaurya Chakra and VCOAS Commendation Card. The Pipes and Drums Band of the Sikh Regimental Centre achieved first position in Adjutant General's Band Display Competition for the year 2021, during his command.
