Athletics and Traditional Sports

Title: Athletics and Traditional Sports
Category: Athletics and Traditional Sports
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
Athletics was among the Regiment's earliest organised sports. In 1919, XLVII Sikhs competed in Egypt, where Wazir Singh won the veterans' race, Naik Kartar Singh the long jump and Sepoy Ghulla Singh the cross-country. The unit then secured the Indian troops' Challenge Cup at Alexandria and sent a team to the Empire Championship at Aldershot. At the first Asian Games in New Delhi in 1951, Havildar Milkha Singh won discus gold and Sepoy Ajit Singh took bronze in the 3,000 metres. Subedar Major Mohinder Singh later won hop-step-and-jump gold and represented India at the 1956 Melbourne Olympics, while Subedar Major Daljit Singh won the 800 metres at Tokyo. Naik Hakam Singh, distinguished in the 20-kilometre walk, received the Dhyan Chand Award on 29 August 2008. Quoit throwing, once practised using the traditional Sikh steel weapon, gradually disappeared and survived only as part of ceremonial dress.
