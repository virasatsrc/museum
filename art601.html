XIV Ferozepore Sikhs

Title: XIV Ferozepore Sikhs
Category: Regimental History
Regiment: Sikh Regiment

The XIV Ferozepore Sikhs, also known as the Regiment of Ferozepore, was formally raised on July 30, 1846, by Captain G. Tebbs after the First Anglo-Sikh War. Civil Commissioner Sir Henry Lawrence and Ensign J. Brasyer recruited disbanded Khalsa Army soldiers, gathering four hundred men south of the Sutlej River. Initially wearing red tunics with yellow facings, the regiment successfully lobbied to wear traditional turbans instead of regulation caps. Lord Gough presented the King’s Colours on December 24, 1846, before the unit received percussion muskets in 1847. The regiment served in Agra, Meerut, and Patna, providing a stabilizing presence. In 1922, it was re-designated as the 1st Battalion, 11th Sikh Regiment, and later renamed the 1st Battalion, The Sikh Regiment in 1945. In 1981, as part of the mechanization plan, this illustrious unit was converted to the 4th Battalion, The Mechanised Infantry.
