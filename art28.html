LIEUTENANT GENERAL PS VADEHRA, PVSM (April 1985 – August 1989)

Lieutenant General Prem Singh Vadehra, PVSM was commissioned into 2 SIKH on 10 June 1954. He commanded 2 SIKH during the 1971 Indo-Pak War and later commanded 11 Mountain Brigade and 19 Infantry Division in Jammu & Kashmir.

As Lieutenant General, he commanded 1 Corps and subsequently served as Chief of Staff, Western Command. He was appointed Colonel of the SIKH Regiment in April 1985, serving till August 1989. For his outstanding leadership and strategic contributions, he was awarded the Param Vishisht Seva Medal (PVSM).
