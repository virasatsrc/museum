LIEUTENANT GENERAL PS VADEHRA, PVSM (April 1985 – August 1989)

Lieutenant General Prem Singh Vadehra, PVSM was commissioned into 2 SIKH on 10 June 1951. He commanded 2 SIKH from December 1966 to December 1968. After the first command, he was selected to command 8 SIKH from April 1972 to May 1973, after the Indo-Pakistan War of 1971.

A keen sportsman, he excelled at cross country and swimming. A graduate from Defence Services Staff College, Wellington, he also attended the Higher Command Course. He was a Brigade Major of a Mountain Brigade, Instructor at Defence Services Staff College, Wellington and Higher Command Wing, College of Combat, Mhow. He commanded 123 Mountain Brigade from 1974 to 1976, 51 Sub Area from 1981 to 1982, and 9 Infantry Division from June 1982 to March 1983.

He took over as Colonel of the Regiment in 1985, and continued so even after his retirement in May 1988. The Regiment passed through some very emotional, critical and difficult years during his tenure, but his perseverance ensured that all challenges were addressed holistically. He voluntarily handed over Colonelcy in August 1989 to Major General Sam Chengapa, AVSM.
