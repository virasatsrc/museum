23 SIKH

The Sikh Regiment

Unit Name: 23 SIKH
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: The Sikh Regiment

Detailed Historical Record

23 Sikh was raised on 1 July 2014 at the Sikh Regimental Centre, Ramgarh. Since its raising, the battalion has carved a special niche for itself in the Regiment through a relentless pursuit of excellence.

The battalion moved to BD Bari, Jammu, in January 2015 and consistently pursued the aim of achieving excellence in the fields of operations and training. Operations-oriented training was conducted throughout the tenure, with particular emphasis on physical fitness, small arms firing and combat shooting.

The battalion also performed exceedingly well in professional and sports competitions. It won the Inter Brigade Hockey Competition, secured 3rd position in the 26 Infantry Division Mortar Competition, 3rd position in the 26 Infantry Division Football Competition, and 4th position in the Boxing Competition.

The battalion subsequently moved to Tangtse, Leh, for its maiden field tenure. During this tenure, it played an important role in maintaining peace and tranquillity along the Line of Actual Control. The unit was involved in frequent face-offs with the People's Liberation Army (PLA), which demanded a high degree of mental and physical robustness, along with determination and presence of mind.

During this tenure, the battalion earned one COAS Commendation Card and four GOC-in-C Commendation Cards.

Owing to meticulous internal procedures and high standards of discipline, the battalion ensured zero military transport accidents and zero untoward incidents during the tenure.

After successfully completing its maiden field tenure, the battalion moved to its new Karmabhoomi at Sri Ganganagar, Rajasthan, amidst the desert dunes. With the blessings of Waheguru, the battalion continues to strive for excellence and aims to establish its presence in its new location by pursuing the pinnacle of excellence in all fields.
