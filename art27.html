MAJOR GENERAL LB SONDHI (June 1982 – January 1985)

Major General Lokhendra Bahadur Sondhi was born on 19 September 1932. He joined the first course of the Joint Services Wing in 1949, and was commissioned in 9 (DECCAN) HORSE on 28 December 1952. He commanded 1 SIKH from April 1970 to December 1972. He was popularly known as 'Marshal' in the Army. He later commanded 43 Infantry Brigade which was converted into 43 Armoured Brigade in 1976. Later, he was Brigadier General Staff of a Corps.

An outstanding sportsman, and a competent professional, he pioneered the conversion of 1 SIKH on Topaz (APCs), and led the Battalion during the Indo-Pakistan War as part of 1 Armoured Division. He also raised the 33 Mechanized Division at Hissar on 15 November 1982.

Major General LB Sondhi was appointed as Colonel of The SIKH Regiment in June 1982 till his death on 24 January 1985, due to a heart attack at Lucknow.
