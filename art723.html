Rashtriya Rifles (SIKH)

Unit Name: 46 RR
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: Rashtriya Rifles (SIKH)

Detailed Historical Record

46 Rashtriya Rifles Battalion (Sikh) was raised at the Sikh Regimental Centre, Ramgarh Cantt, Jharkhand, on 15 September 2001 under Col H. N. Mhaisalakar (later Brigadier).

The unit was entrusted with the tasks of counter-insurgency and counter-terrorism operations, monitoring the internal security situation in the politically active and sensitive town of Baramulla, providing safe passage to Army convoys, and undertaking other operationally related tasks in Baramulla town with effect from 1 June 2002.

The unit left no stone unturned in understanding the operational dynamics of the area and carrying out its assigned tasks in the best possible manner. Since its raising, the battalion has been conferred with one General Officer Commanding-in-Chief, Northern Command Unit Appreciation in January 2009.

The battalion has conducted a series of counter-insurgency operations backed by actionable intelligence, meticulous planning, determined execution and professional acumen. During the period 2017-19, these operations resulted in the elimination of four terrorists, the apprehension of five terrorists, and the apprehension of five over-ground workers.

Through its deep-rooted and proactive intelligence network, the battalion has also been effective in the neutralisation of three militant hideouts and the recovery of a large cache of warlike stores.
