LIEUTENANT CG BAKER, VC

Title: Lieutenant CG Baker, VC
Category: Hall of Fame
Award: Victoria Cross
Era / Action: First War of Independence, 1858
Citation (Written):

Lieutenant CG Baker, VC (First War of Independence, 1858) On 27 September 1858, at Suhejnee, near Perro, Bengal, Lieutenant Baker was in command of a mixed party of cavalry and unmounted police (about 120 strong) which attacked and put to fight a force of about 100 rebels. The whole operation, brilliantly conceived and most gallantly carried out, resulted in the utter defeat of the enemy.
