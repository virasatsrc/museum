CAPTAIN STEWART CC DOUNT, VC

Title: Captain Stewart CC Dount, VC
Category: Hall of Fame
Award: Victoria Cross
Era / Action: First War of Independence, 1857
Citation (Written):

Captain Stewart CC Daunt, VC (First War of Independence, 1857) On 02 October 1857 at Chota Behar, India, accompanied by a Sergeant, acted with conspicuous gallantry in the capture of two guns, particularly the second, which they rushed and took, pistolling the gunners who were moving down the detachment, one third of which was hors-de-combat at the time. On 02 November, the Lieutenant chased mutineers across a plain into a richly cultivated area. He was dangerously wounded while attempting to drive out a large body of rebels from an enclosure.
