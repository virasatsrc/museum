LIEUTENANT GENERAL HARBAKHSH SINGH, PADMA VIBHUSHAN, PADMA BHUSHAN, VrC (April 1953 – September 1971)

Lieutenant General Harbakhsh Singh was born on 01 October 1913 at Village Badrukhan, near Sangrur. After his initial schooling he joined the second course of Indian Military Academy (IMA), Dehradun and was commissioned on 15 July 1935. He saw service on the North-West Frontier during the Mominand Campaign of 1935, and also saw action in NWFP and Malaya. He attended the first long course at Staff College, Quetta after which he was posted as GSO-1 (Operations and Training), Eastern Command.

He was posted as Deputy Commander of 161 Infantry Brigade and conducted the main battle of Shalateng on 07 November 1947. He then led 1 SIKH in operations around Handwara. In 1948, he took over command of 163 Infantry Brigade which successfully captured Tithwal on 18 May 1948 - for his bravery, he was awarded the Vir Chakra. He was Deputy Commandant, Indian Military Academy and Director of Infantry at AHQ. He served as GOC of 27 Infantry Division and 5 Infantry Division and was the COS at HQ Western Command.

When the Chinese invaded in 1962, he was moved from Shimla to take over the command of 4 Corps; later he was side-stepped as GOC 33 Corps. In 1964, he was promoted as an Army Commander and took over as GOC-in-C, Western Command (1964-69), successfully leading during the Indo-Pakistan War, 1965. For his stellar leadership in the 1965 War, he was awarded the Padma Bhushan and later the Padma Vibhushan.

He superannuated from service in September 1969 and passed away on 14 November 1999, aged 86 years. He was Colonel of the SIKH Regiment from April 1953 to September 1971.
