After the War

Title: After the War
Category: After the War
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
The Regiment raised 20 Sikh on 1 March 1976, 21 Sikh on 1 June 1977 and 22 Sikh on 1 September 1980. Operation BLUE STAR in June 1984 caused a grave emotional and disciplinary crisis; 9 Sikh was disbanded, while 3 Sikh and 18 Sikh temporarily became mixed-class battalions before reverting to pure Sikh composition by May 1986. Further expansion followed with 13 Sikh in 1985, 11 Sikh in 1988 and 14 Sikh in 1989, carrying the Regiment into a new operational era.
