Operation MEGHDOOT-Siachen

Title: Operation MEGHDOOT-Siachen
Category: Operation MEGHDOOT-Siachen
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
Indian troops occupied key passes on the Siachen Glacier in April 1984. The first Sikh battalion deployed there was 10 Sikh "Dashmesh," which served on the Central Glacier from April to September 1987 at heights between 16,000 and 20,500 feet. It endured Pakistani firing, extreme cold and hazardous terrain while keeping the enemy under pressure.

20 Sikh served on the Southern Glacier from 17 July 1989 to 1 July 1990. Covering a twenty-seven-kilometre frontage, it destroyed the enemy's Chulung Base Camp and gun position, killing forty-four personnel, and occupied the 17,600-foot Iqbal post through winter. In August 1994, 5 Sikh moved to the Turtuk sector. On 17 May 1995, thirteen men repulsed a larger enemy force attempting to capture an isolated winter post. In 2000-01, 13 Sikh became the Regiment's first battalion on the Northern Glacier. Eighteen Sikh, 20 Sikh during a second tenure, and 4 Sikh also served on this highest battlefield.
