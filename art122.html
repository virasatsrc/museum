Raising of the Training Battalion

Title: Raising of the Training Battalion
Category: Raising of the Training Battalion
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
Under the revised scheme, XXXV Sikhs, earlier listed among the battalions liable for reorganisation or disbandment, became the Training Battalion of the new 11th Sikh Regiment. Orders were received on 26 December 1921, and the battalion began functioning at Jhelum on 1 February 1922. The former XIV King George's Own Ferozepore Sikhs became 1/11 Sikh; XV Ludhiana Sikhs became 2/11 Sikh; XLV Rattray's Sikhs became 3/11 Sikh; XXXVI Sikhs became 4/11 Sikh; XLVII Duke of Connaught's Own Sikhs became 5/11 Sikh; and XXXV Sikhs became 10/11 Sikh, the Training Battalion and future Regimental Centre.

Besides training recruits, 10/11 Sikh maintained personnel records, calculated manpower wastage, administered extra-regimental employment and documented discharged, dismissed or disabled soldiers. It also managed the pay and allowances of VCOs and men. The permanent numbers and titles were formally approved through the Gazette of India, Army Department, dated 1 December 1922.
