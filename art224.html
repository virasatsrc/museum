Hockey-The Regimental Game

Title: Hockey-The Regimental Game
Category: Hockey-The Regimental Game
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
Hockey entered the Regiment in 1900 and became a major tradition after the Punjab Native Army Hockey Tournament began in 1907. XIV Ferozepore Sikhs and XV Ludhiana Sikhs dominated its early years. The Sikh Regimental Centre team was formed at Ambala in 1948 under Colonel M.M. Badshah and Major S.S. Bajwa. Its greatest early triumph came in the 1955 All India Dhyan Chand Tournament, where it defeated Aryan Club, Lahore, largely composed of Pakistan's Olympic players, by 2-0.

The 1962 war interrupted this progress, but Lieutenant Colonel Hari Pal Kaushik, Vir Chakra and Arjuna Award, an Olympian in 1956, 1960 and 1964, helped revive the team. Major H.J.S. Chimni emerged as a leading international forward during 1969-76, and by 1976 the Regimental team had won several major national tournaments. After declining following the move to Ramgarh, the team was revived by Brigadier Kanwaljit Singh in 1992 and Lieutenant General M.S. Bhullar in 1994. By 2007, it had won five of eleven civil tournaments.
