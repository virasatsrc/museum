BRIGADIER RG MATHUR, VSM (May 1981 – June 1982)

Brigadier Ravi Gopal (Baboo) Mathur was born on 20 February 1934 and commissioned on 07 June 1953 into 1 SIKH. He took part in operations in NEFA during 1962 and commanded 1 SIKH during the 1971 Indo-Pak War with great tactical acumen.

He commanded 26 Infantry Brigade and later served as Brigadier General Staff (BGS) at HQ 1 Corps. He served as Colonel of the SIKH Regiment from May 1981 to June 1982. A consummate infantry commander with deep regimental pride, he was awarded the Vishisht Seva Medal (VSM) for his distinguished service.
