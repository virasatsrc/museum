BRIGADIER CS NUGYAL, PVSM, UYSM (February 1986 - December 1986)

Brigadier Chandan Singh Nugyal was commissioned into 6 SIKH on 17 December 1961 and commanded the unit in Counter Insurgency and High Altitude environments. He commanded the Sikh Regimental Centre from February to December 1986. Later rising to Lieutenant General with PVSM and UYSM decorations, his dynamic vision at SRC focused on recruit soldiering ethos, field craft, and sports development.
