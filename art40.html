LIEUTENANT COLONEL MM BADSHAH (October 1947 - August 1948)

Lieutenant Colonel Man Mohan Badshah was commissioned on 01 February 1938 into 2 SIKH. He commanded 2 SIKH from August 1947 to October 1947. He was the first Indian Commandant of SRC. He commanded the Centre from October 1947 to August 1948 and was responsible for settling the Centre at Ambala. The SOPs for the Regimental Centre at Ambala were formalised due to his dedicated efforts. The Regimental Hockey Team was also conceived and formed by him at Ambala.
