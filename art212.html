Ramgarh-The Permanent Home

Title: Ramgarh-The Permanent Home
Category: Ramgarh-The Permanent Home
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
In April 1976, the Centre moved 1,280 kilometres from Meerut to Ramgarh Cantonment. Four special trains departed on 16, 19, 22 and 26 April, the last reaching Barkakana on 29 April. Colonel B.B. Upreti, followed by Colonel K.C. Kalley and Brigadier S.C. Puri, transformed 795 acres of largely barren land into the Regiment's new home. Training resumed within a week, and the 19th Platoon was attested on 24 May 1976.

The Centre trains recruits in companies named after Major Prakash Singh, Captain Gian Singh, Captain Ishar Singh, Captain Karam Singh and Havildar Bachittar Singh. Instruction combines weapons, field craft and physical training with Sikh history, religious values and regimental tradition, culminating in the attestation oath before the Guru Granth Sahib.

Ramgarh gradually acquired the institutions that embody the Regiment's memory: the War Memorial and Quarter Guard, the Regimental Gurdwara, the Sikh Museum re-established in 1978 and developed into a permanent building in 1987, and the Museum-cum-Motivational Hall dedicated on 2 May 1995. The Centre also created training, administrative and sports complexes. Sustained afforestation produced about 300,000 mature trees, while four artificial lakes improved water conservation. From Jhelum to Nowshera, Ambala, Meerut and Ramgarh, the Centre remained the foundation of the Regiment's fighting efficiency and the guardian of its heritage.
