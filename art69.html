BRIGADIER RAKESH RAINA, VSM (January 2017 - January 2020)

Brigadier Rakesh Raina was commissioned into 13 SIKH on 08 June 1985. He served as Second-in-Command and subsequently commanded 13 SIKH. Later, he commanded 67 Infantry Brigade. During his service, he served as GSO-3 (Intelligence) in HQ MP Range, GSO-2 (Operations) in HQ CIF (K), Director Rail & Air in AHQ, Deputy Commander, 33 Mountain Brigade and as Deputy GOC in HQ 54 Infantry Division.

The officer has been awarded Vishisht Seva Medal and VCOAS Commendation Card. The Republic Day Contingent of the SIKH Regiment was adjudged as the Best Marching Contingent for Army Day Parade, during his command.
