LIEUTENANT GENERAL GS SHERGILL, VSM** (November 2013 – October 2015)

Lieutenant General Gursharan Singh Shergill, VSM** was commissioned into 8 SIKH on 11 June 1977. The General Officer has excelled in all mandatory courses, prominent amongst which are Young Officers' Course, BSW, Junior Command, DSSC, Senior Command, CG and HC.

The officer commanded 8 SIKH from March 1997 to April 1999 at Mamun Cantonment. He commanded 93 Infantry Brigade in Operation Rakshak (J&K), 71 Sub Area and 'Romeo' Force in Counter Insurgency environment.

He has had varied staff appointments like Brigade Major of an Infantry Brigade and Chief Staff Officer of the Land Vector at the Strategic Forces Command. As the Commandant WARDEC New Delhi, Brigadier General Staff (Joint Training & Policy) in ARTRAC and DACIDS (Organisation) in HQ IDS AHQ, he was instrumental in formulating and evolving the modern training policy of the Indian Army.

He has been an Instructor at Infantry School (Commando Wing) Belgaum and a Directing Staff at Defence Services Staff College, Wellington.

The General also has the distinction of serving as a United Nations Military Observer, first in Central America and then in El Salvador, from January to September 1992. He was a speaker at the Sri Lanka Army Defence Seminar in 2012 at Colombo, where he delivered a talk on "India's Assistance in Reconstruction and Resettlement."

He took over as the COS, HQ Central Command, and Colonel of The SIKH Regiment in 2013. After 38 years of glorious service, the General Officer hung his boots on 31 October 2015.
