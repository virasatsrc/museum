LIEUTENANT GENERAL GS SHERGILL, VSM** (November 2013 – October 2015)

Lieutenant General Gursharan Singh Shergill, VSM** was commissioned into 8 SIKH on 11 June 1977. He commanded 8 SIKH, 93 Infantry Brigade in Operation Rakshak (J&K), 71 Sub Area, and the elite 'Romeo' Force in Counter-Insurgency operations.

He served as a United Nations Military Observer in Central America and El Salvador in 1992. He served as Chief of Staff, HQ Central Command, and made seminal contributions to Army training policy. He was Colonel of the SIKH Regiment from November 2013 to October 2015, hanging up his boots after 38 years of glorious service. He was awarded the Vishisht Seva Medal with two bars (VSM**).
