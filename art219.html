Reuniting the Regimental Family

Title: Reuniting the Regimental Family
Category: Reuniting the Regimental Family
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
Regimental Reunions bring veterans, serving soldiers, families and future generations together to renew the ethos and shared memory of the Regiment. The first Post-War and Post-Independence Reunion was held at Ambala from 28 February to 2 March 1950. General K.M. Cariappa, Commander-in-Chief, was the Chief Guest, while Major General V.R. Khanolkar led the Regiment. Veterans of the First and Second World Wars, war widows and pensioners from India and Pakistan attended, making the gathering a powerful link between the pre- and post-Independence Regiment.

The second Reunion took place at Meerut on 26-27 December 1955 under Brigadier Harbaksh Singh, Vir Chakra, with Lieutenant Colonel Girdhari Singh as Centre Commandant. Nearly 2,000 pensioners, serving personnel and family members attended. General S.M. Shrinagesh addressed the Regiment, and Defence Minister Dr Kailash Nath Katju inaugurated the Nand Singh Stadium. The third Reunion, held from 17 to 19 March 1961, was marked by the inauguration of the new Gurdwara Sahib by Lieutenant General H.H. Sir Yadvindra Singh, Maharaja of Patiala.
