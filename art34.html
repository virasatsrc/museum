LIEUTENANT GENERAL RS SUJLANA, PVSM, AVSM, VSM (December 2006 – June 2011)

Lieutenant General Rajinder Singh Sujlana, PVSM, AVSM, VSM was born on 05 June 1951 at Ambala Cantonment. He was commissioned into 9 SIKH in June 1971, and as a young officer, took active part in the 1971 Indo-Pakistan War. He has undergone various prestigious army courses at Infantry School Mhow, Defence Services Staff College Wellington, Army War College Mhow and National Defence College, New Delhi.

He commanded 3 SIKH in Jammu & Kashmir and Babina, 77 Mountain Brigade (the world famous Chindits Brigade) in Assam during Operation Rhino, 24 Infantry Division and 10 Corps.

He has been an Instructor at the Indian Military Academy and held various staff appointments including Brigade Major of 323 Infantry Brigade, Colonel General Staff of newly raised Delta Force in Doda (J&K) and at IMA, DDG Army Liaison Cell, DDG Public Information and ADG Public Information. He was Commandant, Indian Military Academy, Dehradun from October 2008 to June 2011.

After retirement, the General Officer briefly served as Chairman, Punjab Public Service Commission and took to writing — he has authored several books on Sikh history and culture and contributes articles regularly to leading national publications and websites.

He assumed Colonelcy of the SIKH Regiment in December 2006 and remained at the helm till his superannuation in June 2011.
