BRIGADIER JG SMYTH, VC

Title: Brigadier JG Smyth, VC
Category: Hall of Fame
Award: Victoria Cross
Era / Action: First World War, 1915
Citation (Written):

Lieutenant JG Smyth, VC (First World War, 1915) On 18 May 1915, near Richebourg L' avoue, France, with a volunteer bombing party of 10 men, Lieutenant Smyth conveyed a supply of 96 bombs to within 20 yards of the enemy's position over exceptionally dangerous ground, after the attempts of two other parties had failed. Lieutenant Smyth succeeded in taking the bombs to the desired positon with help of one of his men (the other nine having been killed or wounded). To achieve his purpose he had to swim a stream, being exposed the whole time to Howitzer, sharpnel, Machine Gun and rifle fire.
