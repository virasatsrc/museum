25 SIKH

The Sikh Regiment

Unit Name: 25 SIKH
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: The Sikh Regiment

Detailed Historical Record

On 28 February 2021, marked a glorious day in the annals of the Indian Army's most decorated and elite SIKH Regiment, when SOORMA BN was raised at The Sikh Regimental Centre, Ramgarh Cantonment under the stewardship of Col Nitish Singh Jaswal. On this momentous day, the Bn took over the mantle of being the youngest Bn of the valiant Sikhs.

Following the successful validation of its raising, the Bn moved to Bhatinda Military Station on 5 September 2021 and was placed under the operational command of HQ 25 Infantry Brigade. During the tenure, the Bn maintained a high state of readiness through regular familiarisation with operational area and active participation in operational discussions. Administratively, the Bn executed all assigned tasks with precision and professionalism, contributing significantly to the overall efficiency and discipline of the station. In acknowledgment of its outstanding contributions and exemplary conduct, the unit was conferred with the prestigious 'Corps Commander's Appreciation', a testament to its unwavering commitment, operational excellence, combat readiness and high standards of discipline.

On 17 June 2024, the Bn formally assumed the operational responsibility of Muguthang Sub Sector in North Sikkim, under command 112 RUDRA Brigade. This deployment marked the Bn's maiden field tenure in a Super High Altitude Area (SHAA). Operating in one of the most challenging terrains, handling the sensitive Naku La Pass on the LAC, the Bn faced extreme climatic conditions, inhospitable geography and a fierce adversary. Despite these adversities, the Bn consistently executed its operational responsibilities with exemplary efficiency, resilience and professionalism. The Bn's performance in this tenure reflected its high standards of preparedness, adaptability and commitment to mission objectives in demanding environments. For its exceptional operational excellence, steadfast dedication and distinguished service in the challenging high-altitude terrain of North Sikkim, the unit was conferred with the 'Chief of the Army Staff Citation', a testament to its enduring spirit and combat readiness making the Bn the youngest ever since raising to be awarded the COAS Unit Citation in its maiden Field Tenure.

The 25th Battalion The Sikh Regiment (SOORMA) has distinguished itself with numerous prestigious accolades including the COAS Unit Citation (2026), Corps Commander's Appreciation (2024), successful patrolling of Naku La on the LAC, validation of VMIMS in Super High Altitude Areas, and championship honors as Winner of the 27 Mountain Division Inter-Battalion 81mm Mortar Competition, Ghatak Competition, and UAS Competition. Living by the immortal motto 'Nischay Kar Apni Jeet Karon', 25 SIKH stands resolute in its devotion to duty and the glorious heritage of the Sikh Regiment.
