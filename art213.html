Symbols of Regimental Honour

Title: Symbols of Regimental Honour
Category: Symbols of Regimental Honour
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
The Colours embody the collective loyalty, sacrifice and heroism of a regiment. Originally carried in battle to identify a unit and provide a rallying point, they became witnesses to victories, losses and acts of devotion in which soldiers often sacrificed their lives to protect them. Made of heavy cloth and bearing the regimental crest and Battle Honours, they are no longer carried into combat but remain consecrated emblems, preserved in Quarter Guards or commanding officers' offices and paraded with full honours on important occasions.
