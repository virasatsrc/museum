North Africa and the Middle East

Title: North Africa and the Middle East
Category: North Africa and the Middle East
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
The Second World War began in 1939, and Sikh battalions soon served across Africa, the Middle East, Europe and Asia. In 1940, 4/11 Sikh joined the 4th Indian Division in Eritrea. At Aqua Col on 12 February 1941, Naik Ujagar Singh attacked an Italian machine-gun position single-handedly and received the Indian Order of Merit. At Mount Samana in March, Captain Mohammad Siddiq led the assault and received the Military Cross; Naik Daulat Singh and Lance Naik Nasib Singh earned IDSMs. After Keren fell, 4/11 Sikh fought Rommel's Afrika Korps at the Omars, Point 204, Derna and Deir-el-Shein. Captain Siddiq received a Bar to his Military Cross, while Lieutenant Colonel J.J. Purves received the DSO after the battalion captured guns, transport, aircraft and thousands of prisoners.

Meanwhile, 2/11 Royal Sikh served in Iraq, Persia, North Africa, Cyprus, Italy and Greece, while 3/11 Rattray's Sikh defended Habbaniya, entered Baghdad on 12 June 1941 and became the first British unit to enter Vichy-held Syria during the mission to Abu Kamal. It later earned the Theatre Honour "Iraq 1941."
