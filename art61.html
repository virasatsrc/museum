BRIGADIER RAJINDER SHARMA (May 1998 - December 2001)

He was commissioned into 17 SIKH (TIGERS) on 21 December 1968. He held instructional appointments at various stages of his career at Infantry School, Mhow. His other appointments include GSO-3 (Intelligence) of an Infantry Brigade, GSO-1 (Operations/ Air) at a Command Headquarters, and Colonel Administration of an Infantry Division. He commanded 17 SIKH, both in Operation Pawan and Operation Rakshak, from September 1988 to July 1992. On promotion, he commanded an Infantry Brigade from May 1986 to May 1988.

His tenure as Centre Commandant is distinguished by the successful conduct of 10th Post War Reunion, held from 09 March to 11 March 2000, besides looking after the welfare of the NOKs of the Kargil martyrs, and rehabilitation of the war disabled.
