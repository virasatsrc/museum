COLONEL TARLOCHAN SINGH KALRA (November 1994 - March 1997)

An alumnus of the 28th NDA Course, Colonel Tarlochan Singh Kalra was commissioned into 2 SIKH on 15 June 1966. He had the unique honour of receiving the Battalion Colours on 02 March 1968. He participated in the 1971 War with the Battalion at Attari-Wagah Border and was Instructor at Indian Military Academy, Dehradun from 1973 to 1975. He was posted to 20 SIKH upon its raising in 1976. After the Staff Course at Wellington, he was posted as DAA&QMG at HQ 26 Infantry Division. He commanded 2 SIKH from March 1985 to September 1988 at Barrackpore and Kaiyan Bowl in Jammu and Kashmir.

His other important appointments include Deputy Commander, 93 Infantry Brigade from where he was specially posted as Commandant, SRC for the Sesquicentennial Celebrations in 1994 which were attended by the COAS. His tenure was marked by inauguration of the Sikh Museum in May 1995, renovation of the War Memorial and refurbishment of the Silver Room in SRC Officers' Mess. The SRC also hosted the Infantry Regimental Centres Conference on behalf of the Infantry Directorate.
