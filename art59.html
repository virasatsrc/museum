COLONEL TARLOCHAN SINGH KALRA (November 1994 - March 1997)

Colonel Tarlochan Singh Kalra was commissioned into 2 SIKH on 15 June 1966 and served in the 1971 War in Jammu and Kashmir. He commanded 2 SIKH during Operation Meghdoot on the Siachen Glacier. He commanded the Sikh Regimental Centre from November 1994 to March 1997, conceptualizing the Virasat Museum, establishing the Regimental War Memorial, and instituting comprehensive sports development programs.
