Fighting in Jammu and Kashmir

Title: Fighting in Jammu and Kashmir
Category: Fighting in Jammu and Kashmir
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
In the Tithwal sector, 1 Sikh was ordered on 24-25 August to capture five posts on the Richmar Gali Ridge. Delta and Charlie Companies seized the Listening Post and PT Ground, while further attacks cleared Pir Sahiba and adjoining positions. Major Somesh Kapur and Havildar Gurdev Singh received the Vir Chakra; Gurdev Singh continued fighting after using his torn turban as a sling. Sepoy Gurmail Singh, who rushed an enemy machine gun, received the Vir Chakra posthumously. The battalion earned three Vir Chakras, three Sena Medals and four Mentions-in-Despatches.

Under Lieutenant Colonel Gulcharan Singh, 3 Sikh defended widely separated posts in the Northern Gallies. Subedar Sunder Singh's small force at Pharkian-ki-Gali repeatedly defeated attacks, while operations completed by 15 October cleared the Mirpur-Bugina Bulge and established control over the Muzaffarabad-Kel route. The advance party of 17 Sikh, led by Major J.C. Joshi, was rushed from Ambala to reinforce the defence at Asal Uttar.

The most costly action fell to 2 Sikh. After capturing eight objectives near Jaurian, it moved to Punch on 29 August for Operation FAULAD and the capture of Raja Picquet. Lieutenant Colonel N.N. Khanna led Alpha, Bravo and Delta Companies through mines, wire and concentrated fire. Junior leaders continued the assault after senior commanders became casualties, and Raja was secured at 0900 hours. Khanna died asking whether the objective had fallen and received the Maha Vir Chakra posthumously. Naik Chand Singh received the Vir Chakra. The battalion gained the Battle Honour "Raja" and Theatre Honour "Jammu and Kashmir 1965."
