XXXVI Sikhs

Title: XXXVI Sikhs
Category: Regimental History
Regiment: Sikh Regiment

XXXVI SIKHS was raised by Lieutenant Colonel Jim Cooke at Jullundur on 23 March 1887 as part of the Bengal Infantry, with eight companies of Jat Sikhs and 225 men from other classes, including Punjabi Mussalmans and tribesmen from the North-West Frontier Province.

Till 1891, the Battalion trained in the vicinity of Jullundur and then moved to Delhi. It participated in the Anglo-Manipur War from 31 March to 27 April 1891 and earned its first Battle Honour, “Manipur,” just five years after its raising.

In 1897, the unit again saw action on the North-West Frontier and fought the epic Battle of Saragarhi.

Following the reorganisation of 1922, the Battalion was redesignated as the 4th Battalion, 11th Sikh Regiment. In 1945, it was renamed the 4th Battalion, The Sikh Regiment.
