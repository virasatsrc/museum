MAJOR GENERAL S CHENGAPA, AVSM (August 1989 – December 1989)

Major General Sam Chengapa, AVSM was commissioned into 2 SIKH in June 1956. He took command of 2 SIKH in May 1972, leading the battalion in critical operational environments. He later commanded 27 Mountain Brigade and 19 Infantry Division in Baramulla.

Promoted to Major General, he commanded 25 Infantry Division and later served in prestigious staff appointments. He served as Colonel of the SIKH Regiment from August 1989 to December 1989. He was awarded the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (AVSM) for his distinguished service to the armed forces.
