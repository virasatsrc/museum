MAJOR GENERAL S CHENGAPA, AVSM (August 1989 – December 1989)

Major General Sam Chengapa, AVSM was commissioned into 2 SIKH in June 1956, and in May 1964 he joined the pioneering team to raise 9 SIKH. The General Officer commanded 9 SIKH from 1971 to 1974. During his command, 9 SIKH fought the 1971 War with Pakistan in Lipa Valley. He was promoted to the rank of Brigadier and commanded 62 Infantry Brigade in 1982. He was Deputy Commandant, Indian Military Academy in 1986. He later commanded 11 Infantry Division. He was awarded the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal in 1990.

Major General S Chengapa, AVSM took over as the Colonel of The SIKH Regiment in August 1989, and remained so till he died on 28 December 1989 at Command Hospital, Pune due to cancer.
