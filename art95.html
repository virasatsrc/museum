End of an Era

Title: End of an Era
Category: End of an Era
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
The Sikh kingdom fell through political betrayal rather than lack of military courage. British commanders acknowledged the Khalsa as the most stubborn and disciplined force they had faced in India. From 1846, and especially during 1857, former soldiers of Ranjit Singh and Hari Singh Nalwa entered British service, making the Sikhs a major source of strength for the British Indian Army.
