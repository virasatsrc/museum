GURU ARJAN DEV JI

Guru Arjan Dev was born at Goindwal on 15 April 1563. During his 25-year stewardship, Amritsar grew eminently to become the seat of Sikh religion. The construction work on Harmandir Sahib was progressed for which Guru Arjan Dev invited Mian Mir, a Sufi Saint of Lahore, who laid the foundation. In 1590, he had another water tank dug at a place about 11 miles South of Amritsar, which he blessed as Taran Taran ('pool of salvation') - it became another important pilgrimage site of Sikhs. He also established new townships such as Kartarpur and Sri Hargobindpur. Guru Arjan brought thousands of Jats of the Majha Belt, who were the sturdiest peasants of Punjab, into the Sikh fold. He compiled the Guru Granth Sahib, which contained the writings of the earlier Gurus and select compositions of poet-saints of all faiths - this became the most powerful factor in spread of Sikh teachings among the masses. He also composed the popular scripture, Sukhmani Sahib ('the psalm of peace'). There was a sudden reversal in the political atmosphere for Sikhs with the advent of Jehangir, who disapproved of their growing popularity and numbers. Guru Arjan Dev was tortured and executed on 30 May 1606 at Lahore. Before his death, he nominated Hargobind, his 11-year old son, as his successor.

'Of all creeds the sovereign creed is to pray to God and do a goodly deed'
Guru Arjan Dev, in Sukhmani Sahib
