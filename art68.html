BRIGADIER KBK KESHAB (October 2014 - January 2017)

Brigadier Kul Bhushan Kumar Keshab was commissioned into 18 SIKH in June 1982. He held various appointments in 18 SIKH and joined 14 SIKH on its raising. He subsequently commanded 17 SIKH (TIGERS) under the most challenging times. He later commanded 51 Infantry Brigade. The officer served as Instructor in Officers Training Academy, Chennai, Military Observer in Angola and as Group Commander in National Security Guards, Manesar.

An outstanding sportsman, he represented the Services in Tennis and reached Services level in Athletics. His experience, talent and clear thought process saw the Centre achieve various milestones like GOC-in-C, Central Command Unit Citation, Best Marching Contingent for Republic Day Parade 2015 and ISO 9001:2008 certification of SRC Records.
