Title: XV Ludhiana Sikhs
Category: Regimental History
Regiment: Sikh Regiment

The XV Ludhiana Sikhs, also known as the Regiment of Loodianah, was raised on August 1, 1846, by re-enlisting disbanded Khalsa Army soldiers from Punjab's Malwa and Majha regions into the East India Company's Bengal Army. Under its first Commandant, Major P. Gordon, the battalion shared a structure similar to the Ferozepore Sikhs, comprising British officers, Viceroy's Commissioned Officers, and other ranks. Organized into ten companies across two wings, its troops were uniquely distinguished by their turbans, which featured nine to ten seams and were super-mounted with a large quoit. In 1922, the battalion was renamed the 2nd Battalion, 11th Sikh Regiment, and later became the 2nd Battalion, The Sikh Regiment in 1945.
