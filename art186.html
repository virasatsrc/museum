Counter-Insurgency and Gallantry

Title: Counter-Insurgency and Gallantry
Category: Counter-Insurgency and Gallantry
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
Across Nagaland, Mizoram and Assam, Sikh battalions conducted Operations ORCHID, BATTLE AXE, BAJRANG and RHINO. In Punjab and Jammu and Kashmir, Operation RAKSHAK demanded prolonged patrolling, ambushes and close combat. Havildar Joginder Singh of 2 Sikh received the Ashoka Chakra posthumously after defeating an ambush in the Zulhami sector. Subedar Surinder Singh of 3 Sikh was awarded the Ashoka Chakra posthumously on 15 August 2002 for successive actions at Jhandidhara and Tarala.

During Operation BLACK TORNADO from 27-29 November 2008, 18 Sikh maintained the cordon around Mumbai's Taj Mahal Palace Hotel for forty-eight hours. On 15 January 1997, 4 Mechanised Infantry (1 Sikh) and 2 Sikh received the title "Bravest of the Brave."
