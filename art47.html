COLONEL GURBAKSH SINGH (June 1966 - July 1970)

Colonel Gurbaksh Singh was commissioned in September 1942 into 3 SIKH, and served in Eritrea and Japan. After Independence, when the SIKH Regimental Centre moved to Ambala, he assumed the appointment of its Deputy Commandant. He later commanded 2 SIKH from 1952 to 1957, 4 SIKH from 1960 to 1962, and then raised 6 SIKH in 1962. He also commanded 14 Infantry Battalion (TA) from 1963 to 1965, before taking over the command of SRC in June 1966. He was a member of UN Mission in China, and AMS to Lieutenant General Harbakhsh Singh during 1965 Operations. As Centre Commandant, he laid stress upon sports and firing standards SIKH Regiment won the National Rifle Association (NRA) trophy in 1968, Jawaharlal Nehru Hockey Championship and were Runners Up in Durand Cup, under his stewardship. During his tenure, 4th Post War Reunion was held and Colours were presented to the Regiment in 1968.
