NAIK NAND SINGH, VC, MVC

Title: Naik Nand Singh, VC, MVC
Category: Hall of Fame
Award: Victoria Cross
Era / Action: Second World War, 1944
Citation (Written):

Naik Nand Singh, VC (Second World War, 1944) On the night of I I March 1944, a Japanese platoon infiltrated through the battalion covering Maungdaw-Buthidaung road in Burma. Naik Nand Singh was commanding the leading section of a platoon which was ordered to re-capture the position at all costs. He led his section up a steep knife-edged ridge under heavy Machine Gun and rifle fire. Although wounded in the thigh, he rushed ahead and captured the first enemy trench by himself with the bayonet. Though wounded again in the face and shoulder by a grenade which burst only a few yards away, he crawled forward and captured the second trench. A short time later, when all the men of his section had either been killed or wounded, Naik Nand Singh dragged himself out of the trench and captured a third trench, bayoneting the occupants. With capture of three trenches, the platoon was able to seize the hill-top and deal with the enemy. Naik Nand Singh personally killed seven of the enemy soldiers. It was due to his determination, outstanding dash and magnificent courage that the position was won back.
