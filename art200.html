HAVILDAR PRAKASH SINGH, VC

Title: Havildar Prakash Singh, VC
Category: Hall of Fame
Award: Victoria Cross
Era / Action: Second World War, 1943
Citation (Written):

Havildar Parkash Singh, VC (Second World War, 1943) On 06 January 1943, at Donnbaid, Mayo Peninsula, Burma, when two Machine Gun carriers had been put out of action, Havildar Parkash Singh drove forward in his own carrier and rescued the two crews under very heavy fire. At the time, the crew Of the disabled carrier had expended their ammunition and the enemy were rushing the two disabled carriers on foot. The NCO's timely and courageous action, entirely on his own initiative, saved the lives of the crew and their weapons. On 19 January 1 943, in the same area, these carriers were put out of action by an enemy Anti-Tank and Machine Gun and lay on the open beach covered by enemy Anti-Tank and Machine Gun fire. One of these carriers was carrying the survivors Of another carrier in addition to its own crew. Havildar Parkash Singh, on seeing what was happening, went out from a safe position in his own carrier and with complete disregard for his own personal safety rescued the combined crew from the disabled carrier, along with the weapons from the carrier. Having brought the crew to safety he again went out on the open beach in his carrier. Still under very heavy Anti- Tank and Machine Gun fire and with the utmost disregard for his personal safety dismounted and connected a tow chain on to the disabled carrier containing two wounded men. Still under fire, he directed the towing of the disabled carrier from under enemy fire to a place of safety. Havildar Parkash Singh's very gallant action, entirely on his own initiatve was an Inspiration to all ranks, both Indian and British.
