MAJOR GENERAL RT MORLIN, AVSM (October 1976 – May 1981)

Major General Ralph Traver Morlin, AVSM, was commissioned into RATTRAY'S SIKH in 1948 and commanded 9 SIKH (MORLIN'S SIKH) during 1964-65. Subsequently, he commanded 17 SIKH, from 1965 to 1966 and 8 SIKH, from 1966 to 1968.

During the 1971 Indo-Pakistan War, he commanded 68 Infantry Brigade, a Corps reserve formation employed to stabilize the situation in Chhamb-Jaurian Sector, and later commanded the 3 Infantry Division.

Major General RT Morlin, AVSM was the Colonel of The SIKH Regiment from 01 October 1976 to May 1981. A keen sportsman and environmentalist, he worked with various NGOs in Canada, in the field of philanthropy and environment, and passed away later.
