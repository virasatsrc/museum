MAJOR GENERAL RT MORLIN, AVSM (October 1976 – May 1981)

Major General Ralph Traver Morlin, AVSM was commissioned into RATTRAY'S SIKH in 1948 and commanded 3 SIKH with distinction. During his distinguished career, he commanded 68 Mountain Brigade and held vital instructional assignments at Defence Services Staff College, Wellington.

As Major General, he commanded 57 Mountain Division in the North-East and subsequently served as Additional Director General of Military Operations (ADGMO) at Army Headquarters. He assumed the Colonelcy of the SIKH Regiment in October 1976 and served till May 1981, bringing progressive modernisation and tactical excellence to the regiment. He was awarded the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (AVSM) for exceptional devotion to duty.
