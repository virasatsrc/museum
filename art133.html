Italy and Victory

Title: Italy and Victory
Category: Italy and Victory
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
In Italy, 2/11 Sikh fought at Umbertide, Aqualagna, Cariano Ridge, Poggio San Giovanni, Onferno, San Marino and the Rubicon Valley. Major Harwant Singh and Jemadar Kaka Singh received Military Crosses. 4/11 Sikh fought at Monte Calvo, Perrarese, the Gothic Line, the Senio and Frassineto, where it repulsed German paratroopers in April 1945. Victory in Europe was celebrated on 7 May 1945.

The Regiment's wartime raisings included 6/11, 7/11, 8/11, 9/11, 14/11 and 15/11 Sikh, the 26th Garrison Battalion and the Sikh Machine Gun Battalion. Their service added Battle Honours including Keren, Omars, Deir-el-Shein, Niyor, Buthidaung, Kanglatongbi, Nyaungu Bridgehead, Irrawaddy Crossing, Coriano, San Marino, Monte Calvo and the Gothic Line. The campaign closed with the centenary celebrations of 1/11 Sikh at Kuala Lumpur in May 1946 and 2/11 Royal Sikh at Bombay in December 1946.
