LIEUTENANT COLONEL GIRDHARI SINGH, VrC, MC (March 1953 - August 1959)

Lieutenant Colonel Girdhari Singh, VrC, MC, had one of the longest and most distinguished tenures as Commandant of the Sikh Regimental Centre, serving at Meerut from March 1953 to August 1959. A highly decorated war veteran who earned both the Military Cross and the Vir Chakra, he was an exceptional administrator, disciplinarian, and sportsman. His leadership left an indelible mark on regimental institutions, training ethos, and infrastructure at Meerut.
