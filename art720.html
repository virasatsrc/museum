24 SIKH

The Sikh Regiment

Unit Name: 24 SIKH
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: The Sikh Regiment

Detailed Historical Record

24th Battalion, The Sikh Regiment was raised under the command of Col Sandeep Vasudeva, SM, on 1 July 2017 at the Sikh Regimental Centre, Ramgarh Cantt.

The raising of the battalion commenced with a strength of 4 officers, 23 JCOs and 339 Other Ranks. After approximately six months at SRC, the battalion was reorganised for its first tenure at Tibri Military Station, with its operational area at Dera Baba Nanak. The "Chaukas Chaubees" moved to Tibri Cantt in January 2018. Col Abhishek Atal took over command of the battalion from Col Sandeep Vasudeva, SM, on 25 June 2018.

Within a short span of time, the battalion oriented itself to its operational role and performed exceedingly well during Exercise Kamyab in November 2018.

The unit also proved its mettle by displaying a high degree of professionalism and competence in all events conducted within Panther Division.

The battalion established a strong presence in various competitions at Division and Brigade levels, securing 1st position in Boxing and Hockey, Runner-up position in Cross Country, and 3rd position in MLP and BFSR competitions at Division level.

The Khalsas of the unit also performed commendably in instructional courses, attaining 14 'I' gradings. The battalion also produced one "Best Student" in the Section Commander Course and one "2nd Best Student" in the JLC Course.

As the youngest battalion of the Regiment, 24 Sikh continues to rise steadily while upholding the ethos and traditions of the distinguished Sikh Regiment.
