18 SIKH

The Sikh Regiment

Unit Name: 18 SIKH
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: The Sikh Regiment

Detailed Historical Record

18 Sikh was raised on 1 July 1948 at Ambala Cantt by Lt Col J. S. Sidhu. The battalion is also known as "Shamsheer", a name given in the 1970s by serving and retired officers of the unit. The name signifies the mighty double-edged Kirpan, symbolising the cutting-edge excellence and high standards maintained by the unit and its personnel since raising.

The battalion was awarded its first COAS Unit Citation for Operation Rakshak, along with the GOC-in-C Unit Citation and the Silver Plaque of the Governor of Jammu & Kashmir for its outstanding tenure in Kanzalwan, J&K.

In 2002, the battalion received its second COAS Unit Citation for Operation Rhino, during its second consecutive field tenure.

The unit's next major operational tenure was in Manipur from 2011 to 2014, where it earned its third COAS Unit Citation, becoming the first infantry battalion of the Indian Army to achieve this rare distinction. In recognition of this achievement, the unit was nominated "Out of Turn" for a United Nations mission in Congo.

During the UN mission in Congo, Shamsheer continued its distinguished record and was awarded the Force Commander's Unit Citation.

The battalion also maintained an impeccable record during peace tenures. It was awarded the GOC-in-C Unit Citation at Pune for its outstanding performance during Operation Black Tornado, conducted in response to the Mumbai terrorist attacks of November 2008, and during the Indo-UK Joint Training Exercise Shamsheer-Bugle.

The unit has also excelled in professional and sporting competitions. It was awarded Best Infantry Battalion of 9 Infantry Division in 1977-78 at Meerut, Best Infantry Battalion of 15 Infantry Division in 1997-98 at Kapurthala, and Best Sports Battalion of 4 Infantry Division in 2003-04 at Faizabad.

18 Sikh is the only infantry unit in the Indian Army with three COAS Unit Citations, two GOC-in-C Unit Citations, a Force Commander's Unit Citation (MONUSCO), and one Governor's Silver Plaque.

A total of 177 honours and awards stand testimony to the strong resolve, dedication and hunger for success of Shamsheer.

The spirit of the battalion is aptly reflected in its enduring belief:

"You are as good as your last job."
