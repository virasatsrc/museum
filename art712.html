16 SIKH

The Sikh Regiment

Unit Name: 16 SIKH
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: The Sikh Regiment

Detailed Historical Record

16 Sikh was raised at Ambala Cantt on 29 January 1948 by Lt Col Jaginder Singh as the "First Infantry Battalion of Independent India", comprising Sikhs drawn from veteran battalions of the pre-Partition Indian Army.

Two companies of the battalion took active part in the Jammu & Kashmir operations of 1947-48, during which Jem Karnail Singh was awarded the Vir Chakra for gallant action and supreme sacrifice.

In November 1962, the battalion was airlifted from Delhi to Tezpur to meet the Chinese threat and occupied defensive positions in the Bomdila Sector.

On 2 May 1968, Lt (later Col) A. K. Chatterjee received the Colours of the Battalion from the President of India, Dr Zakir Hussain, at Meerut.

On 14 October 1987, the battalion was airlifted for Operation Pawan and proved its mettle on foreign soil by helping liberate Jaffna from the LTTE. During the tenure, 78 anti-national elements were killed, while 2nd Lt R. S. Nagar, four JCOs and 38 Other Ranks made the supreme sacrifice.

In 2002, for its stupendous performance in counter-insurgency operations in the Rajouri Sector, the battalion was awarded the COAS Unit Citation.

During its tenure at Ustad (J&K-HAA) from 2005 to 2007, the battalion created a record by neutralising the maximum number of terrorists and recovering a large quantity of arms and ammunition without suffering any casualties. For this achievement, it was conferred the GOC-in-C Northern Command Unit Appreciation in 2008.

For its outstanding performance and achievements during its Pithoragarh tenure, the battalion received the GOC-in-C Central Command Unit Appreciation in 2009.

The unit continued its impressive record and was awarded the GOC-in-C Northern Command Unit Appreciation in 2014 for operations along the Line of Control at Keri, J&K, completed without any casualties.

The battalion moved for Operation Meghdoot in July 2015 and, for its excellent performance during the tenure, earned the GOC-in-C Northern Command Unit Appreciation in 2018.
