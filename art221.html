New Milestones

Title: New Milestones
Category: New Milestones
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
The eighth Reunion, held from 16 to 20 November 1992, included a white-water rafting expedition on the Alaknanda organised by 5 Sikh. The ninth, from 17 to 21 October 1996, marked the Regiment's Sesquicentennial. General S.R. Chowdhury, Chief of Army Staff, presented Colours to 11 Sikh and 14 Sikh, and a 10.8-kilogram silver Sesquicentennial Trophy was unveiled.

At the tenth Reunion, held from 7 to 11 March 2000, the family of Subedar Major and Honorary Captain Sadhu Singh Sangha donated ten lakh rupees for higher professional education of wards of JCOs and other ranks. On 11 March, the formal affiliation of INS Kirpan with the Sikh Regiment was also celebrated. The eleventh Reunion took place from 17 to 20 February 2004; General N.C. Vij visited the Centre and laid a wreath at the War Memorial.

The twelfth Reunion, held from 4 to 8 November 2008 alongside the 33rd Biennial Conference, was presided over by Lieutenant General R.S. Sujlana with Lieutenant General H.S. Panag as Chief Guest. Veterans above seventy were honoured with Tamrapatras, and a Reunion Brochure and Special Day Postal Cover were released. Across twelve gatherings, the Reunions preserved the Regiment's history not as a distant record but as a living inheritance shared by soldiers, veterans and their families.
