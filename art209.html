SUBEDAR SURINDER SINGH, AC

Title: Subedar Surinder Singh, AC
Category: Hall of Fame
Award: Ashok Chakra
Era / Action: Operation Rakshak, 2002
Citation (Written):

Subedar Surinder Singh, AC (Operation Rakshak, 2002) Subedar Surinder Singh was commanding a platoon in Rajouri District of Jammu and Kashmir, deployed on the Line of Control on 09 March 2002. Based on information received, his platoon laid an ambush along the likely infiltration route. The ambush party saw a group of terrorists coming towards them but Subedar Surinder Singh ensured that his party held fire till the militants came close to the ambush party, when he shot the first militant dead. In the ensuing firefight, the other militants melted away in the jungle. Promptly, Subedar Surinder Singh organised a search with his party. While conducting the search, his party came under heavy militant fire from behind a boulder. The JCO charged fearlessly and single handedly killed another militant. The remaining militants sneaked into a nearby village. Subedar Surinder Singh and his party cordoned the village under continuing militant fire. In order to neutralise the militants, the JCO decided to raid the house where the militants were hiding. He exhorted the raiding party, and charged into the house where he received bullet injuries due to militant fire. Despite multiple injuries, the JCO moved ahead and killed two more militants before succumbing to his injuries. The JCO single-handedly accounted for four of the six militants killed in this action. For this display of most conspiCUOUS bravery, Subedar Surinder Singh was awarded the Ashok Chakra, posthumously.
