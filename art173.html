Barki, Khem Karan and OP Hill

Title: Barki, Khem Karan and OP Hill
Category: Barki, Khem Karan and OP Hill
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
On 10 September, 4 Sikh under Lieutenant Colonel Anant Singh attacked Barki on the Lahore axis. Despite heavy artillery and automatic fire, its companies captured the village and destroyed fortified pillboxes. Subedar Ajit Singh received the Maha Vir Chakra, while Major Shamsher Singh, Havildar Ajmer Singh and Lance Naik Pritam Singh received Vir Chakras. The battalion was then ordered into Khem Karan on 12 September without adequate preparation or support. Enemy tanks and an ambush encircled its scattered groups, causing serious losses and captures, but the battalion recovered and returned to Barki until the ceasefire.

In August and September, 7 Sikh countered infiltrators in the Mandi and Betar valleys. It captured Lahori Shah during Operation PHANESHAH on 21-22 September and later joined the brigade assault on OP Hill. Fighting over two days, the battalion scaled mined, precipitous approaches and cleared bunker after bunker in hand-to-hand combat. It lost twenty-one killed and fifty wounded, but won the Battle Honour "OP Hill" and Theatre Honour "Jammu and Kashmir 1965."
