COLONEL HS TOOR (April 1997 - April 1998)

Colonel Harnek Singh Toor was commissioned into 18 SIKH on 14 March 1971. He commanded 18 SIKH from 19 June 1991 to 29 June 1994 at Bikaner, where the unit stood first in sports competitions in 24 Division. After the command tenure, he assumed the appointment of Colonel MS at HQ 16 Corps from 1994 to 1997. Later he became the Commandant of the SIKH Regimental Centre, where he was responsible for the inauguration of the new KLP accommodation for recruits. He superannuated from service on relinquishing the command of SRC. He is currently settled abroad in Canada.
