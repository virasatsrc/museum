4 SIKH

The Sikh Regiment

Unit Name: 4 SIKH
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: The Sikh Regiment

Detailed Historical Record

4 Sikh was raised as XXXVI Sikh at Jalandhar on 23 March 1887 by Lt Col J. Cook.

On 12 September 1897, the battalion fought the legendary Battle of Saragarhi, where Havildar Ishar Singh and 21 soldiers faced approximately 12,000 Orakzai and Afridi tribesmen in the North-West Frontier Province. All 22 soldiers made the supreme sacrifice while defending the Saragarhi Post, establishing an enduring example of fighting to the last man and last round. The defenders were awarded the Indian Order of Merit (IOM), then the highest gallantry award available to Indian soldiers. The Battle of Saragarhi came to be recognised as one of the most celebrated examples of collective bravery.

The battalion has earned 22 Battle Honours, including Barki in 1965 and Siramani in 1971 after Independence. It also fought in the Battle of Walong in 1962 and holds four Theatre Honours.

The battalion served as part of UNIFIL during 2005-06 and was awarded the UNIFIL Force Commander's Unit Citation for its active participation during the Israel-Lebanon conflict.

After completing its tenure in Operation Meghdoot and Eastern Ladakh, the unit moved to Chandimandir in August 2012. In recognition of its splendid performance, the battalion was awarded the Western Command Army Commander's Appreciation on 15 January 2015.
