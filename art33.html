LIEUTENANT GENERAL DEVRAJ SINGH, PVSM, AVSM (June 2004 – November 2006)

Lieutenant General Devraj Singh, PVSM, AVSM was born in November 1946 in Agra District of Uttar Pradesh. He is an alumnus of National Defence Academy and was commissioned into 5 SIKH on 11 June 1967. He has done a number of army courses including Tactical Operations and Staff Course at Frunze Military Academy, Moscow, Staff Course at Defence Services Staff College, Wellington, and Senior Command and Higher Command Courses at College of Combat, Mhow.

General Devraj Singh has held various staff, instructional and command appointments which include GSO-3 (Military Operations) and AMS at AHQ, Senior Instructor and Battalion Commander at National Defence Academy, the prestigious appointment of Military Attaché in Russia and Deputy GOC of a Division in North Eastern Sector. After commanding 5 SIKH, the officer commanded 5 Sector RR, and later commanded a Division in the North Eastern Sector.

For his distinguished service of an exceptional order, he was awarded the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal in 1996 while commanding 5 Sector RR in the Kashmir Valley, and the Param Vishisht Seva Medal on 26 June 2006. The General Officer was the Director General of Infantry before superannuating in November 2006.

Lieutenant General Devraj Singh, PVSM, AVSM was Colonel of the SIKH Regiment from June 2004 to November 2006.
