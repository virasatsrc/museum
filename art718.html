22 SIKH

The Sikh Regiment

Unit Name: 22 SIKH
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: The Sikh Regiment

Detailed Historical Record

22 Sikh was raised on 1 September 1980 at Ramgarh Cantt under the leadership of Lt Col Jagdish Lal Anand. The unit has participated in all major operations, including Operation Blue Star, Operation Wood Rose, Operation Pawan, Operation Rhino, Operation Rakshak, Operation Parakram and Operation Falcon.

During Operation Pawan in March 1988, while executing Operation Vajra, the battalion achieved a major operational success by eliminating LTTE cadres and capturing a large cache of weapons and stores.

In January 2001, in recognition of the brilliant work ethic displayed by the battalion during Operation Vijay and Operation Rakshak, the unit was awarded the COAS Unit Citation.

During Operation Parakram, the battalion was deployed at Dera Baba Nanak and successfully undertook major tasks related to de-mining. During these operations, Maj Harpal Singh made the supreme sacrifice and was awarded the Sena Medal posthumously.

On 9 January 2011, the battalion rescued 19 French nationals who were badly stranded during incessant snowfall in North Sikkim. The rescue was undertaken at short notice and reflected high standards of courage and professionalism. The battalion also played a pivotal role in the facelift of the revered Gurudongmar Shrine, located at an altitude of approximately 17,800 feet, and received considerable appreciation for its efforts.

During the devastating earthquake of 18 September 2011, which severely affected Sikkim, the battalion launched extensive rescue and relief operations. Its efforts received widespread appreciation from the local population and the government.

For its outstanding performance in North Sikkim, the battalion was awarded the GOC-in-C Eastern Command Unit Appreciation on 26 January 2013.
