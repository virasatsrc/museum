INS KIRPAN

Indian Navy (Affiliated to Sikh Regiment)

Unit Name: INS KIRPAN
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: Indian Navy (Affiliated to Sikh Regiment)

Detailed Historical Record

HMS Sikh was sunk off Tobruk in the Mediterranean on 14 September 1942. It was thereafter felt that something should be done to perpetuate the memory of this association, in which much pride had been taken.

After Independence, The Sikh Regiment was extremely keen to continue its linkage with the Indian Navy. It was therefore decided to associate INS Kirpan with the Regiment, and a formal ceremony establishing this new association between the Regiment and the Indian Navy was undertaken.

During the special "Sainik Sammelan" held on 11 March 2000, on the occasion of the 10th Post-War Reunion, Lt Cdr Shiv Kumar, Captain of INS Kirpan, presented a crest of the ship to Lt Gen M. S. Bhullar, Colonel of the Regiment, and in return received the Sikh Regiment Flag.

INS Kirpan is an indigenously designed missile corvette, built by Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE), Calcutta. The ship was commissioned on 12 January 1991 by Vice Admiral V. S. Shekhawat at Calcutta.

On her maiden voyage from Calcutta to Port Blair, INS Kirpan was tasked with a search and rescue mission involving a sailing boat, which she successfully accomplished on 26 January 1991. The ship subsequently sailed towards Visakhapatnam on 10 February 1991 to become an integral part of the Eastern Fleet.

The ship was equipped with a formidable array of weapons and sensors to counter multidimensional surface and air threats. Its armament included long-range anti-ship missiles, surface-to-air missiles and fully automated quick-firing guns. Its sensor suite included surveillance, navigation and fire-control radars, supported by a modern electronic-warfare system.

The ship's administrative authority was the Flag Officer Commanding-in-Chief, Eastern Naval Command, and it operated under the operational command of the Flag Officer Commanding, Eastern Fleet.

INS Kirpan celebrated its anniversary every year on 12 January, commemorating its commissioning and its enduring association with The Sikh Regiment.
