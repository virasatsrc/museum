Operations in Jammu and Kashmir, 1947-48

Title: Operations in Jammu and Kashmir, 1947-48
Category: Operations in Jammu and Kashmir, 1947-48
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
After unfurling the Tricolour at India Gate on 15 August 1947 and at the Red Fort on 16 August, 1 Sikh was chosen for the first airlift to Kashmir. Lieutenant Colonel Dewan Ranjit Rai landed at Srinagar at 0830 hours on 27 October 1947 and secured the airfield before advancing towards Baramulla. At Mile 32 Hill, his 264 men delayed thousands of raiders and gained the vital time required for reinforcements to arrive. Rai was killed while supervising the withdrawal and received the first Maha Vir Chakra of the Indian Army posthumously; 27 October became Infantry Day.

At Shalateng on 7 November, 1 Sikh attacked frontally while Kumaonis and armoured cars of 7 Cavalry enveloped the raiders, killing about 500 and breaking the threat to Srinagar. The battalion recaptured Pattan, Baramulla and Uri by 13 November. It then fought at Nalwar and Bhatgiran, where Jemadar Nand Singh, VC, and Subedar Bishan Singh, MC, were killed and awarded posthumous Maha Vir Chakras; Naik Chand Singh also received the MVC.

In February 1948, under Colonel Harbaksh Singh, 1 Sikh cleared Handwara, Kupwara and Trahagam. Under Lieutenant Colonel Kehar Singh, IDSM, it captured Chowkibal on 21 May and Tithwal on 23 May before assaulting Richmar Gali on 30 May. On 13 October, Lance Naik Karam Singh held a small knoll against repeated battalion-sized attacks despite being wounded, earning the Param Vir Chakra. The battalion received the Battle Honours "Srinagar 1947" and "Tithwal 1948," and the Theatre Honour "J&K 1947-48." 7 Sikh also distinguished itself at Pethahir on 22 March and Shulur on 30 March, earning five Vir Chakras and nineteen Mentions-in-Despatches.
