The Great War-Europe

Title: The Great War-Europe
Category: The Great War-Europe
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
When Germany invaded Belgium on 4 August 1914, India entered the First World War as part of the British Empire. The Indian Expeditionary Force, including the 3rd (Lahore) Division, was diverted from Egypt to reinforce the exhausted British Expeditionary Force in France. XV Sikhs and XLVII Sikhs landed at Marseilles on 26 September 1914, with XV Sikhs becoming the first Indian troops to set foot on European soil. After hurried re-equipment and limited training, the battalion's moved north in October and were committed piecemeal in severe weather against superior German forces.
