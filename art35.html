LIEUTENANT GENERAL SUMER SINGH, AVSM (July 2011 – May 2013)

Lieutenant General Sumer Singh, AVSM was commissioned into 9 SIKH on 15 June 1975. He commanded 9 SIKH in Counter-Insurgency operations in J&K and served as Instructor at the High Altitude Warfare School (HAWS).

He commanded 26 Infantry Brigade on the western borders and 27 Mountain Division in the Eastern sector. As Lieutenant General, he served as Director General of Staff Duties (DGSD) at Army Headquarters. He served as Colonel of the SIKH Regiment from July 2011 to May 2013, bringing strategic insight and welfare initiatives to the regiment. He was decorated with the Ati Vishisht Seva Medal (AVSM).
