LIEUTENANT GENERAL SUMER SINGH, AVSM (July 2011 – May 2013)

Lieutenant General Sumer Singh, AVSM was commissioned into 9 SIKH on 15 June 1975. He joined 20 SIKH in 1976 on its raising and was subsequently empanelled to 22 SIKH in July 1983. After distinguished regimental service and staff appointments in the early years, he graduated from the Defence Services Staff College, Wellington. He has done the prestigious Senior Command, Higher Command and National Defence College Courses.

During his illustrious career, he held a variety of challenging command, staff and instructional assignments. He commanded 22 SIKH in Punjab and Jammu & Kashmir, 80 Infantry Brigade in Jammu & Kashmir and 4 Infantry Division as part of a Strike Corps.

He was an Instructor in Commando Wing Belgaum, Tactics Wing of Infantry School Mhow, and in Indian Military Training Team, Bhutan. He has also been the Military Advisor to the Government of Seychelles. He subsequently served as ADG Military Operations, Director General Perspective Planning at IHQ of MOD (Army), and finally as COS in HQ South Western Command, prior to superannuation in May 2013.

The General Officer settled at Jaipur after retirement where he passed away on 15 July 2023.
