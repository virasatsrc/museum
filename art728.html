12 SQN AF

Indian Air Force (Affiliated to Sikh Regiment)

Unit Name: 12 SQN AF
Category: Unit History
Affiliation: Indian Air Force (Affiliated to Sikh Regiment)

Detailed Historical Record

12 Squadron, Royal Indian Air Force was formed and equipped with Spitfire aircraft on 1 December 1945 at Kohat, under the command of Sqn Ldr N Haider.

In 1946, the Squadron moved to Bhopal and was equipped with Oxford aircraft as its initial flying equipment. It subsequently moved to Mauripur for conversion to C-47 Dakota aircraft, after which it relocated to Chakalala, Rawalpindi, in mid-1947.

In August 1947, the Squadron was transferred to Agra, from where it participated in the Indo-Pak War of 1947-48.

On 27 October 1947, 1 Sikh (now 4 Mech) was airlifted from Delhi (Safdarjung) by C-47 Dakotas of 12 Transport Squadron of the then Royal Indian Air Force and inducted into Srinagar to counter the Pakistani ingress. The troops reached just in time to save the Srinagar airstrip and the city from being overrun by Pakistani militiamen.

The combined effort of 1 Sikh and 12 Squadron played a decisive role in saving the Kashmir Valley and ensuring the territorial integrity of India. This operation was also the first joint operation of Independent India and occupies a significant place in Indian military history. To commemorate this historic event, 27 October is celebrated as Infantry Day.

The affiliation between The Sikh Regiment and 12 Squadron, Indian Air Force, was formally celebrated at the Sikh Regimental Centre, Ramgarh Cantt, on 31 October 2009.

The ceremonial affiliation event included a ceremonial parade, exchange of flags, and the signing of the Affiliation Charter, symbolising the enduring bond between the Sikh Regiment and 12 Squadron.
