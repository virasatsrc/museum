HAVILDAR JOGINDER SINGH, AC

Title: Havildar Joginder Singh, AC
Category: Hall of Fame
Award: Ashok Chakra
Era / Action: Naga Hills, 1956
Citation (Written):

Havildar Joginder Singh, AC (Naga Hills, 1956) Havildar Joginder Singh was escorting a small convoy of three jeeps carrying important stores for a forward post in Phake in Naga Hills. As the leading jeep in which he was sitting was negotiating a bend on the road, hostiles suddenly opened Light Machine Gun fire from a hillock nearby. Though wounded in the right leg, Havildar Joginder Singh, leaving his jeep ran forward to attack the hostiles. He was wounded again in the right shoulder, but he continued his charge undeterred, followed by six other members of his party. When Havildar Joginder Singh was about 25 yards from the hostiles, a Light Machine Gun burst hit him in his stomach and he fell down. Despite his wounds, he continued to crawl forward and threw two grenades on the hostile post and silenced it. Unmindful of his wounds, he continued to inspire his men to advance, who pressed forward and cleared the hostile location. A few moments later he succumbed to his injuries. Inspired by his gallant leadership, Havildar Joginder Singh's men were able to remove the road block which enabled the movement of the much needed stores to reach the forward company in time.
