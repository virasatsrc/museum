Partition and Reconstitution

Title: Partition and Reconstitution
Category: Partition and Reconstitution
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
As the Sikh battalions returned from the Second World War, the demand for Pakistan led to Partition in 1947 and the division of the Indian Army. Punjabi Muslim personnel left Sikh units for Pakistan, while Sikh soldiers from Punjab and Frontier Force regiments joined the Regiment. The Sikh Regimental Centre moved from Nowshera to Ambala. During the communal violence, Sikh units of the Punjab Boundary Force protected Muslim communities around Ambala; Havildar Balwant Singh received the Ashoka Chakra Class III. The first infantry battalion raised in independent India was 16 Sikh, formed by Lieutenant Colonel Jaginder Singh on 29 January 1948. It was followed by 17 Sikh under Lieutenant Colonel N.J.M. Pettangell, MC, on 1 June and 18 Sikh under Lieutenant Colonel Jagjit Singh Sidhu on 1 July.
