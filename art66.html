BRIGADIER HN MHAISALKAR (April 2009 - April 2012)

Brigadier Harshavardhan Narayan Mhaisalkar was commissioned in 17 SIKH (TIGERS) on 15 December 1976. He later commanded 13 SIKH and then raised 46 RR Battalion (SIKH). He held varied appointments to include staff and instructional appointments. He is a graduate of Jawaharlal Nehru University, and also attended Staff Course, Senior Command Course at Mhow, and HDMC course at CDM, Secunderabad.

He held varied appointments, including GSO-2 in IHQ of MOD (Army), Instructor at Infantry School, Mhow and Defence Services Staff College, Wellington, and International Liaison Administration Officer at High Commission of India in UK. He was the Deputy Director General Infantry (A) at IHQ before coming to SIKH Regimental Centre as Commandant. He retired on 30 April 2012, after an outstanding tenure as the Commandant of the SIKH Regimental Centre.
