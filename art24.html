MAJOR GENERAL SATINDER SINGH, PVSM (September 1971 – October 1976)

Major General Satinder Singh, PVSM was commissioned on 01 June 1948 into 1 SIKH. He participated in the 1947–48 J&K Operations and in the 1962 Sino-Indian conflict in NEFA. He commanded 1 SIKH with distinction during the 1965 Indo-Pak War.

He later commanded 120 Infantry Brigade and served as Deputy Commandant & Chief Instructor at the Indian Military Academy, Dehradun. Promoted to Major General, he commanded 25 Infantry Division and subsequently served as Chief of Staff, HQ Southern Command. He took over as Colonel of the SIKH Regiment in September 1971, guiding the regiment through the momentous 1971 Indo-Pak War until October 1976. For distinguished service of the most exceptional order, he was awarded the Param Vishisht Seva Medal (PVSM).
