True to Salt-1857

Title: True to Salt-1857
Category: True to Salt-1857
Artist: Sikh Regimental Centre
Year: Historical
During the uprising of 1857, the new Sikh units remained "true to salt." XIV Sikhs secured Allahabad Fort, fought through Cawnpore and joined the relief of Lucknow. Their conduct earned the Battle Honour "Lucknow," and the regiment retained the red turban worn during the campaign. At Arrah, from 27 July to 3 August, Jemadar Hukam Singh and fifty-one men of Rattray's Sikhs defended Arrah House against nearly 8,000 mutineers and rebels. Hukam Singh received the First Class Indian Order of Merit, eleven men received Third Class awards, and the unit gained "Defence of Arrah."
