BRIGADIER IS GAKHAL (June 2004 - September 2006)

A third-generation soldier and an alumnus of Indian Military Academy, Dehradun, Brigadier Inderjit Singh Gakhal was commissioned into 1 SIKH in December 1973. A keen sportsman, he excelled in Hockey, Basketball and Golf. During his long and illustrious career, the officer held varied appointments to include Instructor at National Defence Academy, Khadakwasla, DAA&QMG of a Mountain Brigade, Colonel MS at a Corps Headquarters and Colonel Administration of an Infantry Division. He commanded 20 SIKH on the Line of Control and later a Rashtriya Rifles Sector in active Counter-Terrorist Operations, where he was grievously wounded. A graduate of the prestigious LDMC Course, he is also the recipient of one COAS Commendation Card and two GOC-in-C Commendation Cards. The officer is an avid reader and often dubbed as 'the encyclopedia of Sikh Regimental history'.

His tenure at SRC was remarkable for introduction of modern training methodologies and pioneering efforts for publication of various hand books, précis and co-curricular activity brochures. Sainik Parivar Manoranjan Kendra and Soldiers' Club were established during his tenure, which provide all facilities of an institute to jawans and their families. Various Regimental Sports Teams were also revived with his encouragement, backing and motivation.
