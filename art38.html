LIEUTENANT GENERAL PGK MENON, PVSM, UYSM, AVSM (01 February 2020 – 31 May 2024)

Lieutenant General P Gopalkrishna Menon, PVSM, UYSM, AVSM was born on 11 May 1964 in Agra. After his pre-commissioning training from IMA Dehradun, he was commissioned into 17 SIKH on 14 December 1985. The General Officer has excelled in all mandatory courses, prominent among which are BSW, Junior Command Course, DSSC, Senior Command, Higher Command and APPPA. He also holds an M.Phil in Defence & Management Studies and Social Science, and a Master's Diploma in Public Administration.

During his illustrious career he had two instructor tenures at IMA Dehradun and was also a Directing Staff at DSSC Wellington. He was part of United Nations Missions three times, holding the appointments of Military Police Officer (Mozambique), Military Liaison Officer (Angola) and Military Observer (Congo).

He commanded 46 RR in Counter Insurgency Operations at Baramulla and 163 Brigade in Narian (J&K). He was GOC of 71 Infantry Division at Missamari from July 2018 to August 2019 and subsequently Corps Commander of 14 Corps from October 2020 to January 2022. He also held the prestigious appointment of Military Secretary at New Delhi.

The General Officer assumed Colonelcy of the SIKH Regiment on 01 February 2020 and remained at the helm till his superannuation on 31 May 2024.
