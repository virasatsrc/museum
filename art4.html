Category: Religious Corner
Artist: Historical Figure
Year: 1534 – 1581
GURU RAM DAS JI

Guru Ram Das was born on 24 September 1534 at Lahore to Hari Das and Mata Daya. He had already spent the better part of his 40 years in the service of the Sikh community when he was chosen as its Fourth Guru. He shifted his base from Goindwal to Guru-ka-Chak on a land allotted to his wife by Emperor Akbar. He widened a natural pool at this site and started building a new town around it which later on flourished into the religious capital of the Sikhs - Amritsar. Guru Ram Das also composed hymns which were later incorporated in the collection of sacred writings - his compositions in Var Gauri refer to many incidents in the life of the Third Guru. He is also remembered in the Sikh tradition for expanding the 'Manji organization' (a Sikh religious administrative unit) for clerical appointments and donation collections to theologically and economically support the Sikh movement. He appointed his own son, Arjan Mal, as his successor. Thus, unlike the first four Gurus who were not related through descent, the fifth to tenth Sikh Gurus were the direct descendants of Guru Ram Das. He died on 01 September 1581 at Goindwal.

'As one lamp is lighted from another, so the Guru's spirit will pass into him and will dispel the darkness in the world'
Guru Ram Das
